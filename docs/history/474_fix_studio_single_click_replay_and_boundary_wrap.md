# 474. Fix Studio Single-Click Replay and Boundary Wrap

## Problem
In `@acc/studio`, when playback played through to the end of the timeline and naturally stopped, clicking **Play** (or pressing Spacebar) required **two clicks** to restart playback. The first click appeared to do nothing (or reset the playhead needle to frame 0), while the second click was required to actually start playback.

---

## Root Causes

1. **Circular `onScrub` Re-Entrancy**:
   When `setSpeed(1)` detected that the playhead was parked at the timeline boundary (`cur >= maxBound`), it invoked `options.onScrub(0)`. In `App.tsx`, `handleScrub` was designed for user-initiated scrubs and included logic to auto-pause active playback and trigger scrub audio feedback. This created an event loop conflict between `handleScrub` and `WebAudioEngine.setShuttleSpeed(1, 0, ...)`.
2. **Clock Stale Initial Frame Abort**:
   Inside the clock advancement `createEffect`, reading `getCurrentFrame` without boundary protection could read the final frame before the synchronous seek propagated to all reactive contexts. Consequently, `DeterministicTimelineClock` initialized at `initialFrame = maxBound` (e.g. 299). On the very first animation frame (16ms later), the clock detected `targetFrame >= endFrame` and immediately halted playback (`setSpeed(0)`), causing Click 1 to abort on frame 1.
3. **Double `onScrub` Dispatch during Natural Auto-Stop**:
   When the RAF loop reached `endFrame`, line 168 called `options.onScrub(discreteFrame)` while `isPlaying` was still true, causing `handleScrub` to re-enter `shuttle.setSpeed(0)` and emit unwanted scrub audio noise at the end of playback.

---

## Plan & Solutions

1. **Synchronous Boundary Wrap Protocol**:
   - In `useShuttleEngine.ts` (`setSpeed`):
     - When starting forward playback at or beyond `maxBound` (`newSpeed > 0 && cur >= maxBound`), synchronously seek `PlayheadController.getInstance().seek(0, true)` and set `cur = 0`.
     - When reversing from frame 0 (`newSpeed < 0 && cur <= 0`), synchronously wrap to `maxBound`.
     - Do NOT dispatch `options.onScrub` during internal boundary wraps; let `PlayheadController.seek(0, true)` handle high-frequency DOM translation and low-frequency Solid signal synchronization cleanly.
2. **Defense-in-Depth Clock Initialization**:
   - In `createEffect`, compute:
     `const initialFrame = currentSpeed > 0 && rawFrame >= maxBound ? 0 : rawFrame;`
   - Explicitly guarantee the clock initializes with frame `0` when starting forward playback from the boundary, completely eliminating the first-tick abort condition.
3. **Clean Auto-Stop at Boundary**:
   - In `tick()`, when `targetFrame >= endFrame && currentSpeed > 0`, seek to `discreteFrame` and call `setSpeed(0)` without intermediate `options.onScrub` recursion.
   - Maintain `options.onScrub(snapped)` exclusively inside `setSpeed(0)` for discrete pause synchronization after `isPlaying` is set to false.
