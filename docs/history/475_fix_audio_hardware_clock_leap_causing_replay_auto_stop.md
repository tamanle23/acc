# 475: Fix Audio Hardware Reference Clock Leap Causing Replay Auto-Stop

## Problem
After playback finished at the end of the timeline (frame 399), clicking Play required two clicks to restart playback.

## Root Cause
In `apps/studio/src/lib/shuttle/useShuttleEngine.ts`, `setSpeed(1)` mutated `setSpeedState(newSpeed)` before calling `WebAudioEngine.setShuttleSpeed(...)`. In SolidJS, signals immediately trigger effects synchronously, instantiating `DeterministicTimelineClock` with the stale `playbackStartAudioTime` from the prior session (e.g. 10.5s ago). When the first RAF frame arrived 16ms later, `audioNow - audioStartSec` was 10.5 seconds (300+ frames), immediately leaping past `endFrame` and auto-stopping back to speed 0 within 16ms. Click 1 appeared to do nothing, and Click 2 worked because Click 1 refreshed `playbackStartAudioTime`.

## Changes Made
1. **`apps/studio/src/lib/shuttle/useShuttleEngine.ts`**:
   - Reordered `setSpeed`: Call `WebAudioEngine.setShuttleSpeed(...)` before `setSpeedState(newSpeed)`.
2. **`apps/studio/src/lib/audio/WebAudioEngine.ts`**:
   - In `stopPlayback()`: synchronously reset `this.playbackStartAudioTime = 0`.
3. **`packages/core-isomorphic/src/math/clock/DeterministicTimelineClock.ts`**:
   - In `tick()`: Added defense-in-depth re-anchor guard if `audioElapsed > 0.5s` on the initial frame.
4. **`autogenerated-tests/studio_single_click_replay_boundary.test.ts`**:
   - Added TC-82-06 verifying initial frame drift re-anchoring.
