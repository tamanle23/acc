# 422. Studio Viewport In-Place Text Editing, Sibling Smart Guides & HUD Anchor Tool

## Problem
Studio Viewport interactions suffered from three usability and capability limitations:
1. **Intrusive Modal Text Input**: Double-clicking text sequences opened a modal popup (`InlineTextEditorModal.tsx`) in the center of the viewport with a dark backdrop, breaking direct-manipulation editing and obscuring canvas context.
2. **Missing Sibling Alignment Guides**: While `MagneticSnapEngine` supported canvas center and safe margins, it had no awareness of sibling sequences active at the playhead needle. Users could not magnetically snap elements to other titles, lower thirds, or shapes on other tracks.
3. **Passive Pivot Reticle & Obscure Hotkeys**: The center pivot reticle crosshair (`+`) was an unmovable decoration, preventing custom rotation/scaling origins. Advanced NLE behaviors (snapping bypass, orthogonal movement lock, center scaling) lacked discoverability and relied on hidden modifier hotkeys.

## Plan
1. **In-Place Canvas Text Editor**:
   - Replace `InlineTextEditorModal.tsx` with `InPlaceTextEditor.tsx`, a zero-backdrop multi-line textarea aligned with the target's bounding box and transform.
   - Configure <kbd>Enter</kbd> to insert newlines, and blur / <kbd>Escape</kbd> / <kbd>Cmd+Enter</kbd> to commit.
2. **Sibling Smart Guides & Snapping**:
   - Extend `MagneticSnapEngine.snap(...)` to harvest bounding boxes of all visible sibling sequences and snap to closest matching edge or center.
   - Render cyan dashed lines between aligned objects.
3. **Interactive Anchor Tool & Tether Line**:
   - Make the center reticle crosshair draggable via handle type `'anchor'`.
   - Render a dashed SVG tether line connecting geometric center to the displaced pivot point.
4. **HUD Status Chips & Rotated Cursors**:
   - Expose interactive status chips on the HUD badge: `[Tool: Move | Anchor]`, `[Snap: ON/OFF]`, `[Axis: Lock/Free]`, `[Scale: Center/Corner]`.
   - Implement `getRotatedCursor` for all 8 handles.
