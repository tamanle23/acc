# 481: Unified Media Asset Conformance Engine (Visual, Temporal, Acoustic, Color)

## Problem
In video editing and rendering pipelines, media assets ingested by users originate from diverse devices with arbitrary specifications:
1. **Visual Spatial**: Non-standard dimensions, vertical aspect ratios (9:16), cropped regions, and anamorphic non-square pixel aspect ratios (PAR) on standard 16:9 timelines. Naive rendering causes letterboxing distortion, non-uniform squishing, or severe moiré downsampling artifacts.
2. **Visual Temporal**: Mixed frame rates (24fps, 25fps, 60fps) on 30fps timelines. Naive frame index stepping causes video playback speed drift and audio desynchronization.
3. **Color Space & Dynamic Range**: 10-bit HDR (HLG, PQ, Rec.2020) and camera log footage blitted to standard SDR Rec.709 timelines with blown-out highlights or washed-out shadows.
4. **Acoustic**: Mixed sample rates (44.1 kHz, 96 kHz) and multichannel layouts (mono, 5.1 surround) mixed into 48 kHz stereo. Naive resampling creates ultrasonic foldback aliasing; naive channel mixing creates clipping or acoustic power imbalance.

## Plan
1. Define canonical conformance types (`FitMode`, `NormalizedCrop`, `SpatialConformanceConfig`, `ColorConformanceConfig`, `AudioChannelMatrix`, `AssetConformanceProfile`) in `@acc/ir`.
2. Enhance `AssetMetadataProber` and `BrowserAssetMetadataProber` with Mediabunny container probing for FPS, display dimensions, PAR, and audio channel configurations.
3. Implement `SpatialConformanceHelper` supporting `cover`, `fit`, `stretch`, `center`, normalized sequence `crop`, and non-square PAR.
4. Integrate progressive power-of-two downsampling (mipmap pyramid) into `IRCanvasCompositor` with RAII scratch canvas cleanup.
5. Implement `TemporalCadenceResolver` with hybrid forward-step ($\le 250\text{ ms}$) vs hardware GOP seek ($> 500\text{ ms}$) thresholding.
6. Implement `AudioConformanceEngine` with ITU-R BS.775-1 5.1/mono downmixing and band-limited Kaiser-windowed Sinc resampling with $\omega_c = \min(1.0, \text{ratio})$ Nyquist cutoff scaling and zeroth-order Bessel expansion ($I_0$).
7. Implement `ColorConformanceEngine` with Rec.2020 to Rec.709 linear 3x3 matrix conversion, ACES Filmic tone mapping, and CSS preview filters.
8. Validate with 6 comprehensive test suites in `/autogenerated-tests/` and verify production build.

## Walkthrough
1. **Spatial Conformance**: When an asset is placed on a sequence, `SpatialConformanceHelper.calculateDrawMapping` computes `(sx, sy, sw, sh)` from normalized `crop` and `(dx, dy, dw, dh)` for canvas placement according to `fitMode` (`cover`, `fit`, `stretch`, `center`) and pixel aspect ratio.
2. **Mipmap Pyramid**: If the downscale ratio exceeds $2.0\times$ (e.g. 8K photo on 1080p canvas), `IRCanvasCompositor` acquires scratch canvases from `UniversalCanvasProvider` and steps down by powers of 2, releasing all intermediate canvases in `finally`.
3. **Temporal Sync**: `TemporalCadenceResolver` computes exact media timestamps: $t_{\text{media}} = t_{\text{trimIn}} + \frac{F - F_{\text{start}}}{\text{FPS}} \times \text{speed}$. Forward stepping is used when $\Delta t \le 250\text{ ms}$, invoking keyframe seeking only when scrubbing backwards or jumping $> 500\text{ ms}$.
4. **Audio Conformance**: Multichannel audio is conformed to stereo via ITU-R BS.775-1 matrix. Downsampling from 96 kHz to 48 kHz uses band-limited Kaiser sinc resampling with $\omega_c = \min(1.0, \text{ratio})$ cutoff scaling to eliminate foldback aliasing.
5. **Color Conformance**: ACES Filmic tone mapping and Rec.2020 $\to$ Rec.709 conversion smoothly map HDR and log footage to SDR range.
