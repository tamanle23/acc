## 1. Context & Motivation

In Iteration 13, per user architectural directive, the visual storytelling studio timeline was upgraded with battle-tested industrial libraries to support large scale timeline editing, instant audio waveform decoding, and frame-accurate industrial NLE scrubbing:
1. **Large Timeline Virtualization**: Elimination of DOM bloat when rendering 100+ tracks and 1,000+ sequences.
2. **WaveSurfer Waveform Rendering**: Peak caching and responsive rendering via `wavesurfer.js`.
3. **Frame-Accurate J-K-L Shuttle Engine**: Discrete integer frame advancement (`frame = Math.round(timeSec * fps)`), multi-speed shuttle state machine (`[-8x, -4x, -2x, -1x, 0x, 1x, 2x, 4x, 8x]`), discrete single-frame stepping (Arrow Left/Right), and hardware audio pitch preservation up to 2x with safe muting at >=4x or in reverse.

## 2. Key Architecture & Deliverables

1. **Package Dependencies**:
   - `apps/studio/package.json`: added `@tanstack/react-virtual: ^3.13.0` and `wavesurfer.js: ^7.8.0`.

2. **Vertical & Horizontal Timeline Virtualization** (`apps/studio/src/components/Timeline.tsx`):
   - TanStack `useVirtualizer` with `count: tracks.length`, `estimateSize: () => 48`, and `overscan: 4`.
   - Horizontal sequence viewport windowing with 400px safety buffer while permanently retaining actively dragged sequence clips (`activeDragState?.seqId === seq.id`) to preserve `@dnd-kit` drag session integrity without unmounting.
   - Pinned sticky track headers (`sticky left-0`), sticky timecode ruler, and playhead needle.

3. **Industrial Waveforms** (`WaveSurferWaveform.tsx` & `AudioWaveform.tsx`):
   - Implemented `WaveSurferWaveform` using `wavesurfer.js` with global peak caching (`Map<string, number[]>`) to prevent redundant audio peak re-calculations across zoom or scrub actions.
   - Integrated into `AudioWaveform.tsx` for seamless backward compatibility.

4. **Frame-Accurate J-K-L Shuttle Engine** (`useShuttleEngine.ts` & `shuttleTypes.ts`):
   - Pure discrete frame math: `snapToDiscreteFrame`, `timeToDiscreteFrame`, `discreteFrameToTime`.
   - Shuttle state machine with J (reverse: `[-1x, -2x, -4x, -8x]`), K (stop: `0x`), L (forward: `[1x, 2x, 4x, 8x]`), Space (play/pause), and Arrow Left/Right (discrete single frame jumps).
   - Audio clock synchronization in `WebAudioEngine.ts` (`setShuttleSpeed`): preserves pitch at 1x and 2x speeds (`preservesPitch = true`), safely mutes Web Audio gain in reverse (`< 0`) or high speeds (`>= 4x`), and restarts audio cleanly from `currentFrame` to prevent float buffer drift.
   - Centralized transport keybindings in `useShuttleEngine` and decoupled editing tool hotkeys in `Timeline.tsx` (eliminating duplicate Space/Arrow keydown firing).
   - Defensive timeline boundary handling: halts at frame 0 (reverse) and `totalFrames` (forward), and restarts from 0 if forward play is initiated at the end.
   - Integrated into both `App.tsx` and `Timeline.tsx`.
