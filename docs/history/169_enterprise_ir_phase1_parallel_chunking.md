## 1. Context & Motivation
Under single-threaded execution, rendering a 2-minute 1080p60 video with complex 3D depth, particle physics, and audio ducking via the Universal Video IR engine took approximately 4 minutes (240s). To make the platform production-ready and satisfy the **Enterprise Transformation Framework** (`docs/brainstorming/ENTERPRISE_GRADE_TRANSFORMATION_FRAMEWORK.md`), we needed to scale rendering horizontally across all available CPU/GPU cores while strictly enforcing:
1. `DETERMINISTIC-HANDSHAKE-BACKPRESSURE` (bit-identical frame math and Merkle root parity).
2. `ZERO-ALLOC-FRAME-LOOP` (zero heap cloning per frame, pre-allocated C memory for native FFI).

---

## 2. Technical Implementation

### A. High-Throughput `IRChunkWorkerPool` (`packages/ir/src/workers/IRChunkWorkerPool.ts`)
- **Keyframe-Aligned Temporal Slicing**: Slices the compiled timeline into $N$ contiguous chunks:
  $$\text{Chunk Size} = \left\lceil \frac{\text{Total Frames}}{\text{Concurrency}} \right\rceil$$
- **Isolated Multi-Worker Instances**: Each worker executes an isolated `IRMasterRenderer` instance with its own `FrameBackpressureLock` and isolated `ModifierPluginRegistry`, seeded deterministically to guarantee bit-identical frame math.
- **Merkle Root Cryptographic Parity**: Each chunk worker computes an SHA-256 chunk hash; the master orchestrator calculates a combined Merkle root:
  $$\text{RootHash} = \text{SHA256}(\text{ChunkHash}_1 \parallel \text{ChunkHash}_2 \parallel \dots \parallel \text{ChunkHash}_N)$$
- **Decoupled Audio Handling**: Workers render pure video streams (`skipAudio: true`), while master voiceover/BGM is mixed once asynchronously, preventing redundant audio decodes.

### B. Lossless FFI Fast Chunk Remuxing & Multiplexing
- **`FFIChunkConcatMuxer` (`packages/ir/src/workers/FFIChunkConcatMuxer.ts`)**:
  - Remuxes intermediate chunk video files at the packet level via pure `bun:ffi` without re-encoding frames ($<500\text{ms}$).
- **`FFAudioMuxer` (`packages/core/src/ffmpeg-ffi/FFAudioMuxer.ts`)**:
  - Implemented lossless stream multiplexing combining the stitched video stream with master WAV audio via pure `bun:ffi` FFmpeg bindings.
  - Properly managed output `AVIOContext` (`avio_close` handle extraction) preventing memory leaks and segmentation faults.

### C. CLI Integration
- Extended `apps/cli/src/commands/ir-render.ts` and `apps/cli/src/index.ts`:
  - Added `-j`, `--concurrency`, `--threads` flags to `ir-render`.
  - When `concurrency > 1` is requested, automatically routes through `IRChunkWorkerPool` with real-time chunk and frame progress logging.
