## 1. Problem Statement
To realize the full architecture of the Video Intermediate Representation, the engine needed:
1. A deterministic headless execution engine (`IRMasterRenderer`) enforcing discrete frame stepping ($t \in \mathbb{N}$), zero wall-clock references, bit-identical frame SHA-256 reproducibility, synchronous backpressure handshakes (`FrameBackpressureLock`), and asynchronous decoupled audio mixing (`DecoupledAudioMixer`).
2. An interactive visual editing preview engine (`IRPreviewEngine`) providing sub-16ms random-access playhead seeking and proxy Level-of-Detail (LOD) downscaling.
3. Verification of strict functional parity between `story-config.json` and the compiled IR timeline, with runtime integration via the `--use-ir` / `useIRBackend` flag in `StoryOrchestratedRenderStage`.
