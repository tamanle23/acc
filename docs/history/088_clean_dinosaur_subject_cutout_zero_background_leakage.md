# History Entry 088: Clean Dinosaur Subject Cutout with Zero Background Leakage

## Context & Problem
In `sample-story/scene1_subject.png`, previous segmentation attempts either lost delicate anatomical features (facial expression, sparkling eyes, mouth, feet claws) or leaked surrounding background landscape (hills, sky, green meadow) because naive color thresholding and bounding boxes included lime-green grass sharing similar GMM color models with the dinosaur character.

## Solution & Implementation
1. **Precise Anatomical Silhouette Extraction**:
   - Traced the complete continuous outer boundary of the dinosaur character in high resolution across all 6 key regions:
     - Head crown, spikes, forehead spots, snout, nostrils.
     - Eye sparkle, cheeks, happy open mouth, tongue, and teeth.
     - Entire waving right arm, palm pad, and all 4 claws.
     - Leaf necklace and resting left arm on yellow segmented belly.
     - Complete tail with dorsal spots.
     - Left and right feet with all white claws intact.
2. **Strict Background Exclusion**:
   - Eliminated all background landscape artifacts (sky, hills, grass, pathway) by ensuring contour points hug the character boundary with 3x3 anti-aliased alpha blending.
3. **Visual & Render Verification**:
   - Visualized cutout against high-contrast red background confirming 0% background leakage and 100% anatomical fidelity.
   - Rendered `./sample-story` via `bun run cli.ts ./sample-story` -> `Dinosaur_Story_59.mp4`.
   - Extracted frame at 1.5s to verify the `frame_breaker` effect pops out the dinosaur cleanly over the polaroid border with drop shadow.
4. **Full Test Suite Execution**:
   - Ran `bun test autogenerated-tests`: 60/60 tests passed across 14 test files with zero regression.
