# 405. Studio-Grade Color Correction Stage & Multi-Tab Color Inspector

## 1. Problem Statement
Professional video color workflows (DaVinci Resolve, Baselight, Adobe Premiere Lumetri) strictly mandate that **Primary Color Correction** (neutral balance, Planckian white balance, exposure, lift/gamma/gain, highlights/shadows rolloff) must precede **Creative Color Grading** (3D LUTs, creative curves, vibrance, vignettes).

Applying a creative 3D LUT directly to unnormalized footage causes color distortion, crushing skin tones into mud and introducing color artifacts. Furthermore:
1. `ColorGradingPlugin` previously lacked a primary color correction phase and intensity blending for 3D LUTs.
2. The Studio Inspector rendered flat numerical input fields, lacking the industry-standard 3-Way Color Wheels (Lift, Gamma, Gain, Offset) and bipolar Kelvin/tint sliders expected by video colorists.

## 2. Plan & Architecture
1. **Primary Color Correction Pipeline (`LutApplicator.ts` & `ColorGradingPlugin.ts`)**:
   - Implement `applyColorCorrectionAndGrading` executing in single-pass pixel traversal over `ImageData`:
     - **Stage 1 (Primary Correction)**: Planckian White Balance ($T, t$), Photometric Exposure ($2^{EV}$), 3-Way Color Wheels (Offset, Lift, Gain, Gamma in ASC CDL space), Highlights/Shadows soft rolloff, Whites/Blacks dynamic anchors, Contrast with $18\%$ mid-gray pivot ($P = 0.435$).
     - **Stage 2 (Creative Grade)**: 3D LUT tetrahedral interpolation with **Linear Intensity Blending ($0\% - 100\%$)**, Smart Vibrance, and Master Saturation.
     - **Stage 3 (Finishing)**: Smoothstep radial vignette with midpoint and feathering.
2. **Dedicated Studio Color Inspector (`StudioColorGradingInspector.tsx`)**:
   - Provide a 4-tab segmented workspace:
     - `[Correction]`: White balance Kelvin (Ice Blue $\leftrightarrow$ Amber) and Tint (Green $\leftrightarrow$ Magenta) gradient tracks, Exposure EV, Contrast, Pivot, Highlights, Shadows, Whites, Blacks.
     - `[Wheels]`: 4 interactive SVG chromatic wheels (Lift, Gamma, Gain, Offset) with radial saturation, draggable pucks, master luminance slider, and double-click reset.
     - `[Creative LUT]`: LUT look selector with style descriptions, **LUT Intensity slider ($0\% - 100\%$)**, Saturation, and Vibrance.
     - `[Vignette]`: Amount, Midpoint, and Feather sliders.
3. **Inspector Wiring (`DynamicModifierInspector.tsx`)**:
   - Mount `StudioColorGradingInspector` automatically when `modifierType === 'vfx/color_grading'`.
   - Update default parameters in `modifierCatalog.ts`.
4. **Formal Verification Suite**:
   - Extended `autogenerated-tests/lut_colorgrading_integration.test.ts` with Suite 4 asserting temperature color shifts, EV multiplication, 50% LUT intensity blending, and studio schema coverage.
