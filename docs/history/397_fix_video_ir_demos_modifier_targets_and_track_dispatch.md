# 397: Fix Video IR Demos Modifier Targets & Track-Level Subtitle Dispatch

## Problem
Following the introduction of the strict 4-domain single-target modifier taxonomy in Iteration 55 (`targetScope: 'standalone' | 'sequence' | 'track' | 'timeline'`), several production Video IR showcase demos generated schema errors when compiled in the Studio or validated via `VideoIRSchemaValidator.validate()`:
1. `complexSciFiDemoIR.ts`:
   - `seq-cyber-spotlight` and `seq-chromatic-burst` on video track `track-v5` were configured with `type: 'overlay'`, but contained modifier `vfx/partial`. Because `vfx/partial` has `allowedSequenceTypes: ['video', 'image']`, validation failed with:
     ```
     Modifier "vfx/partial" on sequence "seq-cyber-spotlight": Modifier "vfx/partial" does not permit sequence type "overlay" (allowed: video, image)
     ```
   - `seq-dialogue-1`, `seq-dialogue-2`, and `seq-dialogue-3` on subtitle track `track-subtitles`, and `seq-hud-callout` and `seq-hud-callout-warning` on callout track `track-hud-data`, directly declared `timed_text/style` on sequence level. Because `timed_text/style` is a stream-level typography modifier with `targetScope: 'track'`, validation failed with:
     ```
     Modifier "timed_text/style" on sequence "seq-dialogue-1": Modifier "timed_text/style" targetScope is "track", cannot be applied to sequence
     ```
2. `astroTelemetryDemoIR.ts` & `quantumPhysicsDemoIR.ts`:
   - Both demos contained `timed_text/style` on individual subtitle sequences (`seq-astro-caption-p*` and `seq-caption-part*`) instead of on the parent `timed_text` track stream.
3. Studio State Commands:
   - `UpdateModifierCommand` and `RemoveModifierCommand` only searched `seq.id === this.sequenceId` within child sequences, preventing track-level modifiers (such as `timed_text/style`) from being dynamically mutated or removed on the track level via the command pipeline.
   - `UpdateTrackCommand` did not snapshot or update `track.modifiers`.

## Plan
1. **Demo Document Alignment (`apps/studio/src/lib/ir/`)**:
   - In `complexSciFiDemoIR.ts`:
     - Update `seq-cyber-spotlight` and `seq-chromatic-burst` to `type: 'video'` and modifier `target: 'video'`, ensuring full compatibility with video track `track-v5` and `vfx/partial`.
     - Move `timed_text/style` to `track-subtitles.modifiers` and `track-hud-data.modifiers`.
     - Cleanly remove sequence-level `timed_text/style` modifiers from dialogue and callout cues.
   - In `astroTelemetryDemoIR.ts` and `quantumPhysicsDemoIR.ts`:
     - Move `timed_text/style` to `track-t1-*-subs.modifiers`.
     - Remove sequence-level `timed_text/style` modifiers and ensure `cues: [...]` is explicitly defined on each sequence.
2. **Defensive Legacy Migration (`apps/studio/src/lib/ir/irState.ts`)**:
   - Enhance `migrateLegacyModifiers`:
     - Auto-repair `vfx/partial` on `overlay` sequences in `video` tracks by promoting `seq.type = 'video'`.
     - Auto-migrate sequence-level `text/style` or `timed_text/style` on `timed_text` tracks to `track.modifiers`.
3. **Command Architecture Expansion (`apps/studio/src/lib/commands/`)**:
   - Expand `UpdateModifierCommand` and `RemoveModifierCommand` to recognize `track.id === this.sequenceId` and manage `track.modifiers` with full undo/redo state preservation.
   - Expand `UpdateTrackCommand` to include `modifiers?: ModifierNode[]` in `UpdateTrackProperties`, `previousSnapshot`, `execute`, and `undo`.
4. **Test Suite Synchronization (`autogenerated-tests/`)**:
   - In `studio_context_aware_inspector.test.ts`: update karaoke active-word test to mutate `timed_text/style` on `track-subtitles`.
   - In `modifier_targets_dynamic_sync.test.ts`: add Test Vector 7 verifying that all 6 studio demo IR documents (`complexSciFiDemoIR`, `astroTelemetryDemoIR`, `quantumPhysicsDemoIR`, `defaultDemoIR`, `broadcastNewsDemoIR`, `cyberAudioVisualizerDemoIR`) validate with zero schema errors.
