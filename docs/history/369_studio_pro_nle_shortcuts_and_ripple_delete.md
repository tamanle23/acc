# History Entry 369: Studio Pro NLE Shortcuts & Ripple Delete (Phase 4)

## Problem
In broadcast video editing environments, timeline trimming and clip deletion require atomic gap closure (Ripple Delete) across single and multi-track timelines without corrupting synchronized audio/video pairs or violating locked track constraints. Furthermore, industry-standard editing efficiency demands tactile keyboard shortcuts: Razor blade toggle (`C`), playhead slicing (`Cmd+K` / `S`), multi-track ripple deletion (`Shift+Delete`), and precision J-K-L shuttle transport with K+L / K+J discrete step scrubbing.

## Plan
1. Upgrade `RippleDeleteSequenceCommand.ts` with multi-track ripple (`rippleAllUnlocked`), companion linked A/V clip discovery (`editorial/av_link` and `linkId`), locked track immunity, and full undo/redo state inversion.
2. Implement `RippleDeleteCommand.ts` conforming to `TimelineCommand` for dispatch through `TimelineCommandDispatcher` and `IRStateManager`.
3. Enhance `useShuttleEngine.ts` to support K+L (single-frame step forward) and K+J (single-frame step backward) key combinations while preserving J-K-L speed acceleration ($1\times \to 2\times \to 4\times \to 8\times$) and K stop.
4. Bind `Cmd+K` / `Ctrl+K`, `Shift+Delete`, and `Delete` (contextual) in `Timeline.tsx`.
5. Create comprehensive formal verification in `autogenerated-tests/timeline_ripple_delete.test.ts`.
