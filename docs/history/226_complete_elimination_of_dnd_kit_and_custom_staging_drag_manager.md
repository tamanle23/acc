# History Record 226: Complete Elimination of @dnd-kit and Implementation of StagingDragManager

## Problem
In `apps/studio`, the global staging pipeline (dragging presets and media assets from `LeftSidebar.tsx` into the Preview canvas viewport or Timeline track lanes) relied on the master `<DndContext>` and `<DragOverlay>` in `App.tsx` and `useDroppable` hooks in `Preview.tsx` and `DroppableTrackLane.tsx`. This prevented the complete removal of `@dnd-kit` packages and added top-level context re-renders during asset dragging.

## Plan
1. Engineer `StagingDragManager.ts` providing spatial hit-testing (`document.elementsFromPoint`) and drag session coordination.
2. Refactor `LeftSidebar.tsx` (`DraggablePresetCard` and `DraggableAssetCard`) with `@neodrag/react`.
3. Eliminate `useDroppable` from `Preview.tsx` and `DroppableTrackLane.tsx`, replacing it with `useStagingDrag()`.
4. Remove `<DndContext>` and `<DragOverlay>` from `App.tsx`, providing a hardware-accelerated ghost overlay.
5. Remove `@dnd-kit/core` and `@dnd-kit/utilities` from `apps/studio/package.json` and prune `pnpm-lock.yaml`.
6. Author unit tests in `autogenerated-tests/staging_drag_manager.test.ts`.
7. Verify all tests and production build.
