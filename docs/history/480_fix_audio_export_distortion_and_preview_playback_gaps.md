# 480. Fix Audio Export Distortion and Preview Playback Gaps

## Problem
Users reported two severe audio defects in the Studio and VideoIR rendering pipelines:
1. **Audio in exported video was "twisted" / distorted**:
   - **Mono-to-Stereo Planar Packing Corruption**: In `MediabunnyClientStrategy` and `MediabunnyServerStrategy`, when extracting mono audio chunks (`numChannels === 1`), `interleaved` was created with length `numFrames * 1` and appended to `sampleChunks`. The final stereo buffer was allocated with `totalExtractedSamplesPerChannel * 2`. It set each chunk with `offset += chunk.length`, which packed mono samples contiguously into the first half of the stereo buffer. Downstream stereo unpacking interleaved adjacent samples into Left and Right channels, causing mono audio to play at **2x speed, +1 octave pitch, phase-jumbled across ears, with the second half silenced**.
   - **Source Sample Rate Erasure**: The audio extractor ignored `sample.sampleRate` and hardcoded `targetSampleRate: 48000`, causing standard 44.1 kHz audio files (MP3/WAV) to bypass resampling and play 8.84% too fast with high-pitch warping.
   - **Unconditional `Math.tanh()` Limiter Overdrive**: `UniversalAudioMixer` unconditionally compressed the master bus with `Math.tanh()`, introducing noticeable odd-harmonic distortion on already mastered audio files.
   - **Synthetic 0.25 Ducking Energy**: `rebuildDuckingProfile` hardcoded 0.25 synthetic voice energy across the entire sequence duration, ducking background tracks by -12 dB even during silence.
2. **Preview silent delay and dropped sequences**:
   - **Un-awaited Async Preload + 5s Silent Buffer Scheduling**: `WebAudioEngine.startPlayback()` fired un-awaited `preloadAssets()`, immediately mixing a 5-second buffer of zeroes and playing it, forcing the user to endure a 5-second silent delay before audio sounded.
   - **Hardcoded 6s Lookahead Ceiling**: `seq.startFrame <= (fps * 6)` prevented any sequence starting past 6 seconds from ever being preloaded.
   - **16 MB Cache Choke-Point**: `AudioAssetCache` capped memory at 16 MB. A single 45-second stereo track occupies ~15.9 MB, causing consecutive tracks to continuously evict each other in an infinite reload loop.

## Plan
1. **Mediabunny Client & Server Demuxing Normalization**:
   - Standardize all audio sample extraction in `MediabunnyClientStrategy` and `MediabunnyServerStrategy` to output stereo interleaved `Float32Array(numFrames * 2)`.
   - For mono audio (`numChannels === 1`), duplicate each sample into Left and Right channel pairs `[mono, mono]`.
   - Detect and return the genuine source sample rate (`sample.sampleRate`) so downstream `DecodedPcmSource` can resample with zero pitch or speed warping.
2. **Transparent Master Bus Limiter & Real Voice Ducking**:
   - In `UniversalAudioMixer.mixStereo`, replace unconditional `Math.tanh(x)` overdrive with linear unity pass-through below 0 dBFS ($\le 1.0$), applying soft proportional gain reduction only when multi-bus sums exceed full scale.
   - In `rebuildDuckingProfile`, query real audio waveform energy from `clip.audioSource` when ready, removing false synthetic ducking.
3. **AudioAssetCache Expansion & Dynamic Lookahead Pool**:
   - Increase `AudioAssetCache` budget to 128 MB (32 entries).
   - In `WebAudioEngine.syncFromIR`, replace the 6-second restriction with a dynamic sliding window around the active playhead (`[currentSec - 2s, currentSec + 30s]`), scanning both audio tracks and video tracks with audio.
4. **Reactive Hot-Swap for Instantaneous Preview**:
   - In `WebAudioEngine.ts`, when `AudioAssetCache` finishes decoding while playback is active, hot-swap the active audio buffer at current context time, completely eliminating the 5-second silence.
5. **Verification**:
   - Implement tests in `autogenerated-tests/audio_export_and_preview_parity.test.ts`.
   - Run all audio regression suites and verify production studio build.

## Walkthrough
1. **Export Mono and 44.1 kHz Audio**:
   When an MP3 or mono voice recording is exported, `MediabunnyClientStrategy` extracts mono frames and duplicates them into interleaved stereo pairs `[mono, mono]`. The detected native rate (e.g. 44,100 Hz) is returned, allowing `DecodedPcmSource` to resample to 48,000 Hz. The audio plays at exact 1x speed with full duration, centered stereo image, and zero pitch shift.
2. **Master Bus Audio Transparency**:
   Uncompressed or pre-mastered music tracks retain 100% of their dynamic range without `Math.tanh()` clipping or harmonic distortion below 0 dBFS.
3. **Instantaneous Playback Preview**:
   When playhead starts from any frame (including past 6s), `syncFromIR` preloads active proximate clips. If an asset is decoding, `hotSwapActivePlayback` replaces the silent buffer as soon as decode finishes, eliminating silent delays.
