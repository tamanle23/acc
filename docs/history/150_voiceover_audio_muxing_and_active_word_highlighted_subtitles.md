# Task 150: Voiceover Audio Track Muxing & Active Word-Highlighted Subtitles in Video IR Pipeline

## Problem Statement
In the Universal Video IR final MP4 export pipeline (`ir-render` / `IRMasterRenderer` / `FFIVideoEncoderSink`):
1. **Missing Voiceover / Audio Track in Final Output:**
   - `FFIVideoEncoder` requires an existing audio file on disk during `.initialize()` to allocate `audioStream` and `aCodecCtx` before writing the MP4 container header (`avformat_write_header`).
   - `FFIVideoEncoderSink` was previously deferring audio creation until `.finalize()`, writing a raw `.pcm` file after the container header had already been written with 0 audio streams, and FFmpeg could not parse headerless raw PCM.
   - `DecoupledAudioMixer` was synthesizing dummy calibrated tones rather than reading real voiceover assets from `timeline.assets`.
2. **Subtitles Missing Active Word Highlighting:**
   - `IRMasterRenderer.ts` had a primitive subtitle branch that joined active cue words into a single static string with no word-by-word karaoke highlighting, ignoring `activeWordHighlight`, `highlightPreset`, and `wordsPerPhrase` settings.

## Proposed Plan
1. **DecoupledAudioMixer Asset Decoding & RIFF WAV Export:**
   - Decode real voiceover WAV assets directly from `timeline.assets` via standard in-memory RIFF/WAV parsing.
   - Mix samples into `masterBuffer` at `seq.startFrame` offsets.
   - Provide `DecoupledAudioMixer.exportToWav(filePath, mixResult)` to output valid 16-bit stereo PCM WAV files with standard 44-byte headers.
2. **Pre-render Master Audio in `IRMasterRenderer`:**
   - Pre-render audio before initializing `sink`, writing `.temp_<timestamp>_master_audio.wav`.
   - Pass `audioPath: tempAudioWavPath` to `sink.initialize(...)`, allowing `FFIVideoEncoder` to open the WAV file, detect audio streams, allocate AAC encoder, and mux Stream #1 into the MP4 container.
   - Clean up temporary audio files upon completion.
3. **Active Word-Highlighted Subtitle Integration:**
   - Attach `settings.captions` style in `StoryConfigToUniversalIRAdapter` to `track_captions`.
   - Preserve `track.style` through `RelationalDAGCompiler`.
   - Use `BoundedAcousticCaptionStrategy` and `drawCanvasCaptionsFromSnapshot` from `@acc/effect-engines` in `IRMasterRenderer` to render styled, karaoke word-highlighted subtitles.
   - Cache `CaptionConfig[]` in `WeakMap` to adhere to `ZERO-ALLOC-FRAME-LOOP`.
4. **CLI Enhancements & Temporal Slicing:**
   - Support `-i` / `--input` and folder paths in `apps/cli/src/index.ts` and `ir-render.ts`.
   - Support `chunkStart` and `chunkEnd` in `IRMasterRenderer.render(...)`.
5. **Verification & Testing:**
   - Verify all 24 autogenerated tests pass without regression.
   - Render sample story chunk `0 -> 60` and verify MP4 streams via `bun:ffi` FFmpeg probing (Stream #0: Video, Stream #1: Audio AAC).

## Changes Made
- `packages/ir/src/types/VideoIR.ts`: Added `style?: Record<string, unknown>` to `ResolvedTrack`.
- `packages/ir/src/compiler/RelationalDAGCompiler.ts`: Preserved `track.style` when building `ResolvedTrack`.
- `packages/ir/src/adapters/StoryConfigToUniversalIRAdapter.ts`: Attached `settings.captions` to `track_captions.style`.
- `packages/ir/src/renderer/audio/DecoupledAudioMixer.ts`: Implemented `decodeWav`, real asset mixing with gain and ducking, and `exportToWav` writing standard RIFF WAV files.
- `packages/ir/src/renderer/IRMasterRenderer.ts`:
  - Integrated `BoundedAcousticCaptionStrategy` and `drawCanvasCaptionsFromSnapshot`.
  - Added `captionConfigCache: WeakMap<ResolvedSequence, CaptionConfig[]>` for zero-alloc frame loop.
  - Pre-mixed master audio to temporary WAV file when rendering to sink with audio tracks.
  - Added support for `chunkStart` and `chunkEnd` temporal slicing.
- `apps/cli/src/commands/ir-render.ts`: Auto-resolved `story-config.json` inside directories; forwarded `chunkStart` and `chunkEnd` to `renderer.render`.
- `apps/cli/src/index.ts`: Added `-i` and `--input` alias options for CLI parsing.
