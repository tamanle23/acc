# 424 - Harmonize Text, Shape, and Transform Modifier Coordinates & Precision Gizmo Bounds

## Problem
In `apps/studio/src/components/preview/TransformGizmoOverlay.tsx`, selected text and vector shape sequences did not wrap their target objects tightly, and were positioned with an offset (e.g. at 45% Y while text rendered at 50% Y, or at 20% Y for chapter headers while text rendered at 50% Y).

Investigation revealed three root causes:
1. **Origin Disconnect between `text/draw` / `shape/draw` and `transform`**:
   Typography and shape presets declare spatial coordinates on the `transform` modifier (e.g. `normX: 0.5, normY: 0.45` or `0.20` or `0.82`), omitting `normX`/`normY` on `text/draw`. `CanvasHitTester.resolveSequenceBounds` correctly read from `transform.params.normX/normY` (e.g. placing the gizmo at 45% Y), whereas `TextModifierPlugin` and `ShapeModifierPlugin` defaulted to 0.5 (50% Y) when `params.normX/normY` were missing on `text/draw`.
2. **TransformModifierPlugin Anchor Drift**:
   When `normX`/`normY` was specified on `text/draw` or `shape/draw` instead of `transform`, `TransformModifierPlugin` defaulted to 0.5, creating a mismatched anchor pivot.
3. **Text Measurement & Plate Padding Inaccuracies**:
   `CanvasHitTester` used a coarse approximation (`charWidthApprox = fontSize * 0.58`) and added background padding even when no plate was present, leading to bounding box width discrepancies with bold or all-caps typography.

## Plan
1. Update `TextModifierPlugin.renderClipSpace` and `ShapeModifierPlugin.renderClipSpace` to inherit `normX` and `normY` from the sequence's `transform` modifier when omitted from `params`.
2. Update `TransformModifierPlugin.renderClipSpace` to inspect child modifiers (`text/draw`, `shape/draw`) for `normX` and `normY` when omitted on `transform.params`.
3. Enhance `CanvasHitTester.resolveSequenceBounds` with:
   - Dynamic canvas 2D measurement (`measureText`) when DOM/OffscreenCanvas is available.
   - High-precision typographic heuristic accounting for uppercase, digits, punctuation, and `fontWeight`.
   - Accurate plate padding logic that only adds plate padding when `backgroundColor` is present.
4. Add automated test coverage in `autogenerated-tests/studio_transform_gizmo_bounds.test.ts` verifying inherited coordinates across presets.
5. Verify 0 IR schema drift and confirm successful Vite build of `@acc/studio`.

## Changes
- **`packages/ir/src/plugins/text/TextModifierPlugin.ts`**:
  - In `renderClipSpace`, resolve `normX` and `normY` by prioritizing `params.normX/normY`, then falling back to `transParams.normX/normY`, and finally `0.5`.
- **`packages/ir/src/plugins/shape/ShapeModifierPlugin.ts`**:
  - In `renderClipSpace`, resolve `normX` and `normY` by prioritizing `params.normX/normY`, then falling back to `transParams.normX/normY`, and finally `0.5`.
- **`packages/ir/src/plugins/transform/TransformModifierPlugin.ts`**:
  - In `renderClipSpace`, resolve anchor pivot from `params.normX/normY`, falling back to child `text/draw` or `shape/draw` coordinates before `params.anchorPoint` and default `0.5`.
- **`apps/studio/src/lib/hit-test/CanvasHitTester.ts`**:
  - Added singleton measurement canvas context for browser environments.
  - Implemented exact text width and line height calculation matching `TextModifierPlugin`.
  - Harmonized plate vs plain text padding bounds and scaled shape dimension calculation.
- **`autogenerated-tests/studio_transform_gizmo_bounds.test.ts`**:
  - Added tests verifying coordinate inheritance between `transform` and `text/draw` modifiers.
