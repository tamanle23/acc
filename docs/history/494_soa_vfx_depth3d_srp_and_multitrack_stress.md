## 1. Problem Statement & Baseline Diagnosis
1. **AoS Particle Memory Bottlenecks & Stack Churn**:
   - `SIMDParticleComputeEngine` used an interleaved Array-of-Structures (AoS) format (`PARTICLE_STRIDE = 11`, 44 bytes/particle), straddling 64-byte cache lines and preventing auto-vectorized SIMD linear sweeps.
   - `DeterministicParticleField` invoked `ctx.save()` / `ctx.restore()` per particle (1,000–1,500 context stack mutations per frame).
   - `CanvasParticleOverlayPlugin` declared `useSIMD?: boolean` in parameter options but orphaned `SIMDParticleComputeEngine`.
2. **Monolithic `renderDepth3DScene` & Incomplete Crossfade**:
   - `renderDepth3DScene` spanned 320+ lines in `packages/effect-engines/src/depth3d/index.ts`, mixing asset resolution, neural inference, depth slicing, sink streaming, and camera projection (violating Single Responsibility Principle).
   - In `parallax.ts`, uninitialized `p.pulseSpeed` produced `NaN` alpha values during particle rendering.
   - `TransitionCrossfadePlugin` was incomplete, lacking reciprocal curve calculations and acoustic equal-power quarter-sine curves.
3. **Eager Video Decoder Quota Exhaustion in Export**:
   - `IRMasterRenderer.prepare()` eagerly instantiated video decoders for all declared video assets simultaneously without concurrency bounds, risking browser GPU driver `QuotaExceededError` on timelines with multiple video clips.

## 2. Architecture & Implementation Changes

### Track 1: Structure-of-Arrays (SoA) Particle Compute & Zero-Alloc VFX
- **`SoAParticleComputeEngine`** (`packages/effect-engines/src/overlay-effects/simulators/SoAParticleComputeEngine.ts`):
  - Pre-allocated single contiguous `ArrayBuffer` partitioned into 10 parallel `Float32Array` attributes (`posX`, `posY`, `posZ`, `velX`, `velY`, `velZ`, `life`, `maxLife`, `size`, `opacity`) and 1 `Uint16Array` attribute (`colorIdx`).
  - Guaranteed 64-byte CPU cache alignment using 16-float padding ($\text{alignedCapacity} = \lceil \text{maxParticles}/16 \rceil \times 16$).
  - 100% stateless closed-form simulation (`computeStatelessFrame`) for non-linear NLE scrubbing with zero accumulator drift.
  - Batched 4-bin alpha quantization rendering (`renderBatched`) using leading `moveTo()` paths, reducing context state operations from $>3,000$ to $\le 16$ per frame.
- **`CanvasParticleOverlayPlugin`** (`packages/ir/src/plugins/overlay/CanvasParticleOverlayPlugin.ts`):
  - Wired `useSIMD` to route to pre-allocated cached `SoAParticleComputeEngine` instance.
- **Re-exports**: Added `SoAParticleComputeEngine` to `@acc/effect-engines`.

### Track 2: Effect Engine Architectural Polish & Feature Completion
- **Single Responsibility Principle Decomposition**:
  - `DepthScenePreprocessor.ts`: Handles asset loading, subject segmentation, depth estimation, and continuous 3-plane layer slicing (`bg`, `mg`, `fg`) with RAII `dispose()`.
  - `DepthCanvasCompositor.ts`: Stateless frame-by-frame renderer managing camera projection, layer compositing, and subtitle rendering.
  - `MediaSinkStreamer.ts`: Manages `IMediaFrameSink` negotiation, cooperative yield frame loop, and sink finalization.
  - `renderDepth3DScene` in `packages/effect-engines/src/depth3d/index.ts`: Streamlined into a 15-line orchestrator façade.
- **Defect Resolution in `parallax.ts`**:
  - Guarded `pulseSpeed = p.pulseSpeed ?? (1.5 + (p.layer || 0) * 0.5)` to eliminate `NaN` in canvas radial gradients.
- **Equal-Power Crossfade**:
  - In `packages/ir/src/plugins/editorial/TransitionCrossfadePlugin.ts`, implemented `calculateCrossfadeAlpha(progress, mode)` with linear and equal-power quarter-sine curves ($\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1.0$).

### Track 3: Studio Multi-Track Playback & Bounded JIT Video Decoders
- **`BoundedDecoderPool`** (`packages/ir/src/renderer/BoundedDecoderPool.ts`):
  - Strict ceiling (default $\le 4$) on concurrent hardware video decoders.
  - Priority-based and LRU eviction when concurrent track demand exceeds capacity.
- **`IRMasterRenderer` JIT Dynamic Leasing** (`packages/ir/src/renderer/IRMasterRenderer.ts`):
  - Replaced eager video decoder instantiation in `prepare()` with JIT lease acquisition in `renderCanvasFrame`.
  - `renderCanvasFrame` resolves active video sequences on frame $t$ and leases decoders dynamically from `BoundedDecoderPool`.
  - Clean `closeAll()` on render completion and disposal.
