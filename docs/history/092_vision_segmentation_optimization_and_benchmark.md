## 1. Context & Optimizations
We implemented 3 core tuning optimizations across the vision segmentation subsystem:
1. **SAM Models (SAM 2.1 Tiny, SAM ViT-Base, SlimSAM)**:
   - Added negative margin prompt points (label 0 at 4 corners + 4 outer margins) to prevent background leakage.
   - Added positive anatomical prompts (label 1 at crest, head, core, legs, flank).
   - Dynamic `argmax(iou_scores)` mask candidate selection.
2. **RMBG-1.4**:
   - Cubic Hermite `smoothstep(0.15, 0.85, val)` alpha mapping for anti-aliased, studio-grade soft edges.
3. **BiRefNet-general & Cutout Compositing**:
   - Enabled `fp16` execution and continuous alpha composite in `createCutoutRgba`.
