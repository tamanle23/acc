   - Connected `VoiceIsolationCloningStrategy` to valid RIFF/WAVE PCM audio synthesis with content-hash caching in `~/.acc/cache/audio/`.
   - Connected `GenerativeBrollEngine` to content-hash caching in `~/.acc/cache/broll/`, eliminating `Date.now()`.
   - Enhanced `MatchmoveVisionFacade` with multi-frame 3D camera trajectory generation.
   - Enhanced `EditingEngineFacade` with concrete exports in `~/.acc/exports/` complying with `GLOBAL-STORAGE`.
   - Upgraded `CleanPlateEngine` to 3-tier inpainting (fast delogo, temporal patchmatch, AI inpaint).
   - Fixed semantic inversion in `TextBasedEditingEngine` returning true `cutRanges` (deleted words) and implemented transcript ripple cut sequence splitter.
   - Upgraded `ProxyAssetManager` with disk-backed cache in `~/.acc/proxies/` and SHA-256 hashing.
   - Enhanced `SpatialVideoExporter` (MV-HEVC, SBS, anaglyph) and `SpatialAudioAmbisonicsEngine` (4-channel B-format and binaural HRIR).
   - Enhanced `ToneMappingEngine` (ITU-R BT.2390 tone curve mapping) and `VoiceIsolationEngine` (spectral, RNNoise, AI Demucs).
   - Decoupled `ConductorSagaOrchestrator` to `SagaOrchestrator` with backward compatibility alias.
   - Defined vendor-neutral `IWorkflowOrchestrator` port in `packages/core/src/workflow/IWorkflowOrchestrator.ts`.
3. **Phase 3: System Performance Tuning, Zero-Allocation Loops & Unified Time-Space State Machine**:
   - Cached `DataView` over `srcDataC` in `FFIVideoEncoder.ts`, eliminating per-frame JS allocations.
   - Created `NativeBufferPool` in `@acc/core-native` for recycling aligned native frame buffers across frame loops.
   - Created pure `DeterministicPtsEngine.ts` in `@acc/core-isomorphic/src/math/pts/` stripping `CanvasSpace`, `DOMSpace`, and `requestAnimationFrame`.
   - Created `StatelessHashNoise.ts` for 100% deterministic, repeatable coordinate-and-frame hash noise.
   - Created `DeterministicPtsForm.ts` for drawing to `ICanvasContext2D` and exporting `Float32Array` vertex buffers for WebGL.
   - Created `DeterministicGsapPtsProxy.ts` enforcing paused timeline architecture and `lagSmoothing(0)`.
   - Created `TimeSpaceStateMachine.ts` and `createTimeSpaceMachine` supporting multi-track boundary policies (`loop`, `pingpong`, `clamp`, `once`), discrete phase lifecycles (`enter`, `idle`, `exit`), and aspect-aware `SpaceContext` viewport projection.
   - Created `TimeSpaceRegistry.ts` for versioned motion graphic templates (`callout_leader`, `kinetic_title`, `particle_ambient`).
   - Synchronized VideoIR schema: added `"time_space"` modifier to `modifiers.ts`, `video-ir.schema.json`, and `VideoIRSchemaValidator.ts`.
   - Implemented `TimeSpaceModifierPlugin.ts` and wired into `ModifierPluginRegistry`.
   - Refactored `KineticTypography.ts`, `TrackingCalloutEngine.ts`, and `DeterministicParticleField.ts` to compose `createTimeSpaceMachine` and `StatelessHashNoise`.
4. **Phase 4: Enterprise Production Resilience & Observability**:
   - Implemented `RenderCheckpointManager.ts` in `packages/core/src/resilience/` storing chunk metadata in `~/.acc/checkpoints/<jobId>.json` for crash resumption.
