## Context & Objectives
In `apps/studio/src/components/Preview.tsx`, the viewport container previously utilized a hardcoded opaque `bg-slate-950/90` and `border-slate-800` card, causing visual dissonance when users switched between Light Frosted Acrylic and Dark Obsidian or customized their optical glass styles and accent colors in the Preferences modal. Additionally, the in-canvas transform handles, bounding box, status badges, and floating quick transport bar required refinement to align with the enterprise Liquid Glass theme.

## Work Accomplished
1. **Outer Viewport Container Glassmorphism**:
   - Replaced `<Card>` with a rounded-2xl `.glass-panel` featuring specular top rim reflections (`var(--glass-specular)`), backdrop saturation blur, and active drag state glow (`ring-2 ring-[var(--accent-primary)] shadow-[0_0_35px_var(--accent-glow)]`).
   - Integrated `.liquid-ambient-mesh` fluid background refraction layer behind the video canvas.
2. **Top Monitor Status Bar Upgrade**:
   - Converted loose badges into sleek frosted `.glass-card` capsules with crisp borders and dynamic accent highlights:
     - Resolution Capsule (`{width}×{height}`)
     - GSAP Timeline Engine badge with animated glowing accent icon
     - Live Playback State pill with glowing ping pulse (`PLAYING` / `IDLE`)
     - Integrated Play/Pause viewport toggle button (`.glass-button`)
     - In-Canvas selection transform telemetry pill (`X, Y, S, R, [Snap]`)
     - Aspect ratio pill (`16:9 Cinema`)
3. **Floating Viewport Quick Transport Bar Polish**:
