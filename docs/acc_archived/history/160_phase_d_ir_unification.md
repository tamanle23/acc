## 1. Context & Objectives
In Phase D of the Tri-Pillar Architecture, the goal is uniting the production storytelling pipeline with the Canonical Universal Video IR rendering engine:
1. **Lossless Video IR Adapter Upgrades:** Ensure `StoryConfigToUniversalIRAdapter` translates all editing parameters (J/L-cuts, color grading/LUTs, multi-track SFX, and platform safe-zone caption styles) into schema-valid V1.0-Stable Video IR documents without data loss.
2. **Unified IR Rendering Backend Execution:** Connect `GenerativeStoryPipeline`'s rendering stage (`StoryOrchestratedRenderStage`) to execute via `IRMasterRenderer` and `FFIVideoEncoderSink` directly when requested (`useIRBackend: true`), establishing a single unified SSOT for video synthesis.

---

## 2. Implementation Summary

### 2.1 Lossless StoryConfig-to-IR Adaptation (`StoryConfigToUniversalIRAdapter.ts`)
- **J/L-Cut Split Edit Offsets:**
  - Forwarded `videoOffsetMs` from scenes into relational sequence start timing:
    `offsetFrames = Math.round((videoOffsetMs / 1000) * fps)`.
- **In-Memory Color Grading & LUT Nodes:**
  - Extracted scene/settings `colorGrading` and injected a `vfx/color_grading` modifier node containing `lutPath`, `contrast`, and `saturation` directly into visual sequences.
- **Multi-Track Sound Effects (SFX):**
  - Mapped scene `sfx` clips into a dedicated `track_a3_sfx` audio track with precise timing offsets (`offsetFrames`).
- **Platform Safe Zones:**
  - Forwarded `settings.platform` (`tiktok`, `youtube`, etc.) into `track_captions` style attributes to guarantee platform safe-zone margin compliance during Canvas subtitle burns.

### 2.2 Direct IR Backend Routing in `StoryOrchestratedRenderStage.ts`
- Wired `FFIVideoEncoderSink` into `StoryOrchestratedRenderStage.ts`:
  - When `ctx.metadata?.useIRBackend` is active, the pipeline compiles the adapted IR document into a global timeline and renders directly via `IRMasterRenderer` to `outputFilePath`.
  - Encodes the full video with zero intermediate MP4 chunk files on disk and emits bit-identical `cumulativeHash` metadata.
