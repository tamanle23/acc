## 1. Context & Problem
While `BiRefNet-general` delivers world-class subject segmentation out of the box, other lightweight models (`SAM 2.1 Tiny`, `SAM ViT-Base`, `RMBG-1.4`, `SlimSAM`) require specialized parameter tuning and prompt engineering to achieve equivalent boundary sharpness and zero background leakage:
1. **SAM Models (SAM 2.1 Tiny, SAM ViT-Base, SlimSAM)** lack negative background exclusion points (label 0) and use default mask index 0 instead of selecting the maximum IoU score mask.
2. **RMBG-1.4** applies hard binary thresholding instead of continuous sub-pixel alpha smoothstep feathering.
3. **BiRefNet-general** execution on CPU takes ~50s in unoptimized fp32 mode, which needs quantization and threading optimization.

---

## 2. Proposed Design & Optimization Points
1. **Point 1: SAM Parameter & Prompt Optimization**:
   - Inject positive foreground points (head, crest, chest, feet) + negative background exclusion points (label 0 at 4 corners and outer edges) to eliminate background leakage.
   - Implement `argmax(iou_scores)` mask candidate selection.
   - Anti-aliased sub-pixel edge feathering.
2. **Point 2: RMBG-1.4 Quality Maximization**:
   - Continuous non-linear `smoothstep(0.15, 0.85, val)` alpha mapping for soft natural edges.
   - Aspect-ratio preserving letterboxing and bicubic resampling.
3. **Point 3: BiRefNet Acceleration**:
   - Quantized dtype / optimized session config for rapid inference.
