### Context & Problem
During end-to-end rendering verification of multi-scene generative stories (`acc ir-render`) and deep Studio playback profiling:
1. `ICanvasInstance` objects passed to `ctx.drawImage()` crashed native Skia/Napi contexts, violating LSP and forcing manual unwrap casts.
2. Background music/audio tracks lacked a first-class declarative IR construct to span the entire compiled timeline, forcing coupled hacks to `seq_1_vis`.
3. CLI route boundaries were blurred: `ir-render` searched for un-synthesized raw configs instead of acting as a pure, headless debug/render stage.
4. Exporting video required an ad-hoc `--export-ir` flag instead of persisting canonical `video-ir.json` by default.
5. Large media assets loaded full in-memory byte buffers causing heap pressure without universal streaming abstraction.
6. Studio playback lagged due to synchronous 1080p canvas redraws on the React UI main thread and 60 Hz full component reconciliation.

---

### Proposed Design
1. **Transparent Canvas Subtyping**: `wrapCanvasContext` proxies `drawImage` and `createPattern` in `NapiCanvasAdapter` and `WebCanvasAdapter` to transparently unwrap `ICanvasInstance` without call-site boilerplates.
2. **Declarative Timeline Duration Standard (`matchLengthOf: 'timeline'`)**: 3-pass Kahn's topological sort in `RelationalDAGCompiler` dynamically computing timeline bounds and propagating duration to terminal tracks. Full 8-point sync across types, schemas, validators, specifications, adapters, and tests.
3. **Strict Route Isolation**: `acc run <pipeline_id>` orchestrates synthesis and multi-modality generation; `acc ir-render` strictly executes canonical `video-ir.json` and fails fast with actionable tips on raw configs.
4. **Default Video IR Persistence**: `VideoExporter` automatically saves `video-ir.json` alongside `final.mp4`. Completely eliminated `--export-ir` flag.
5. **Universal Streaming Asset Blobs (`IAssetBlob`)**: `AssetProtocolRegistry` resolving `file://`, `data:`, and `http://` streams natively in Node, Bun, and browser workers.
6. **Real Web Worker OffscreenCanvas Preview**: Dedicated `preview.worker.ts` hosting `IRPreviewEngine` via `canvas.transferControlToOffscreen()`, 15 Hz throttled React scrubber synchronization, and adaptive Dynamic LOD (0.5x during playback, 1.0x when parked).
