## 1. Problem Statement & Motivation
- In Scene 1 (`frame_breaker`, `polaroid`), the dinosaur subject cutout previously had visual defects:
  1. The left foot (and claw digits) was partially truncated due to a restricted GrabCut ROI bounding box.
  2. The eyes and mouth cavity were hollowed out/transparent because standard connected component / GrabCut luminance thresholding misclassified dark pupils and mouth interior as background.
  3. The rendered dinosaur character appeared with missing eyes, mouth, and a cut foot.

---

## 2. Root Cause Analysis
- **Hole Creation in Segmentation**: Standard GrabCut classifies different colored/dark internal features (eyes, mouth cavity, belly texture) as `GC_BGD` or `GC_PR_BGD`. Standard morphological closing with small kernels `(5, 5)` was insufficient to bridge these interior regions.
- **Bounding Box Constraints**: The default foreground initialization rectangle was too tight on the bottom margin (`h * 0.70`), clipping the lower paws and feet.

---

## 3. Key Changes Made
1. **Upgraded `VisionEngine` (`apps/python-backend/src/services/vision_engine.py`)**:
   - Implemented External Contour Hole Filling: Replaced simple connected components with `cv2.findContours(..., cv2.RETR_EXTERNAL)` and `cv2.drawContours(solid_mask, [largest_contour], -1, 255, -1)`. This guarantees 100% solid interior opacity for all facial features (eyes, pupils, eyelashes, mouth, teeth, tongue, nostrils, belly plates).
   - Added anti-aliasing edge feathering via `cv2.GaussianBlur` for seamless composition on video frames.
2. **Regenerated `sample-story/scene1_subject.png`**:
   - Applied color-assisted GrabCut with expanded bounding box `[302, 38, 771, 637]` (covering the entire head, dorsal crest, waving arm, belly, and both feet).
   - Filled all internal contours to ensure complete solid rendering of eyes and mouth.
