## 1. Context & Objective
Following the completion of the 6-Phase Universal Auto-Config and PipelineIR integration, the ACC ecosystem had `sample-story` dedicated solely to Pipeline 4 (Generative Storytelling). To ensure complete, practical testability and demonstration parity across all 6 pipelines, a comprehensive suite of sample projects was requested in `./samples/`.

The user explicitly mandated that each pipeline sample must cover:
1. **SFX (Sound Effects)** design cues (`sfx` array, ducking, timing offsets, audio cues).
2. **VFX Overlays** (`overlayComposite`: Camera UI, Digital Glitch, Sci-Fi HUD, Matrix Rain, VHS, Dust/Embers, Confetti, Rain on Glass).
3. **3D In-Depth Effects** (`depth3d`: Isometric Billboard, Isometric Podium, Gyro Focal Tilt, Volumetric Sandwich, Frame Breaker, Cutout Portal, Macro Bokeh).
4. **Triple-emission Artifacts**: `story-config.json`, `pipeline-ir.json` (v3.0.0), and `omnichannel-config.json`.

---

## 2. Key Implementations

### A. Material Synthesis Script (`scripts/synthesize-sample-materials.ts`)
- Implemented lightweight, pure TypeScript synthesis functions:
  - `createWavBuffer`: Generates clean 16-bit PCM mono WAV buffers (sine tones, noise sweeps, sound effects) with attack/release envelopes to prevent audio pops.
  - `createSyntheticImage`: Uses `@napi-rs/canvas` to render high-contrast geometric subjects with foreground card layouts, subtitles, and particle backdrops suitable for 3D depth segmentation.
- Populated `./samples/` with 6 dedicated subdirectories:
  1. `01-scraper-summarizer`: Article Markdown (`breaking_news.md`), RSS feed payload (`source_feed.json`), tech cover PNG, tech BGM, whoosh & notification SFX.
  2. `02-db-templating`: 16-row CSV (`tech_market_adoption.csv`), leaderboard JSON payload (`database_payload.json`), brand logo PNG, synth BGM, data blip & sub drop SFX.
  3. `03-repurposing-recycler`: MP4 video clip (`podcast_clip.mp4`), word-level timestamp transcript (`repurpose_transcript.json`), viral BGM, pop & whoosh SFX.
  4. `04-generative-storytelling`: Multi-scene PNGs, voiceovers, text narrations, cinematic orchestral BGM, cinematic trailer hit & kaiju roar SFX.
  5. `05-rule-logic`: Logic decision tree (`dialogue_logic_tree.txt`), Pythagorean theorem proof JSON (`proof_problem.json`), board diagram PNG, classical BGM, chalk write & success bell SFX.
  6. `06-ambient-loops`: Lo-Fi sleep beat loop MP3, rain sound & tape hiss SFX, ambient texture PNG, shader config (`ambient_config.json`).

### B. Effects Calibration & Artifact Synchronization (`scripts/calibrate-sample-effects.ts`)
- Calibrated `story-config.json`, `pipeline-ir.json`, and `omnichannel-config.json` across all 6 samples:
  - Injected explicit `depth3d` modes (`isometric_billboard`, `isometric_podium`, `gyro_focal_tilt`, `volumetric_sandwich`, `extruded_text_3d`, `macro_bokeh`).
  - Injected `overlayComposite` configurations (`camera_ui`, `sci_fi_hud`, `vhs`, `dust`, `confetti`, `rain_on_glass`).
  - Injected `sfx` cue arrays with `duckBgm` flags and `sfxMarkers` into `audioComposition`.

### C. Prober & Classifier Fine-Tuning
- **`ScriptProberHandler.ts`**: Excluded `README.md` files from being categorized as spoken voiceover/narration scripts to prevent documentation from skewing automated heuristics.
- **`RuleLogicClassifierStrategy.ts`**: Added support for mathematical proof, theorem, and decision tree keywords (`theorem`, `proof`, `q.e.d.`, `qed`, `problemStatement`) scoring up to 98% confidence.
- **`RepurposingClassifierStrategy.ts` & `GenerativeStoryClassifierStrategy.ts`**: Supported discriminating between native `story-config.json` files and bridge-adapted artifacts generated by `AutoConfigLegacyBridgeAdapter`.

### D. Automated E2E Verification (`autogenerated-tests/samples-scaffolding-verification.test.ts`)
- Created comprehensive test suite verifying:
  - 100% asset existence and integrity across all 6 sample directories.
  - Media probe discovery and accurate classification with $\ge 85\%$ confidence for each pipeline.
  - Complete `depth3d`, `overlayComposite`, and `sfx` validation against schemas and engine registries.
  - Universal Video IR v3.0.0 AST integration in `pipeline-ir.json`.
  - Non-destructive dry-run execution via `AutoConfigEngineFacade.scaffoldDirectory`.
