# 165: Visual Parity & Full Effect Synchronization in Canonical Video IR Pipeline

## 1. Context & Motivation
During Phase C and Phase D testing of the Universal Video IR transition (`acc ir-render` and unified `VideoEditPipelineOrchestrator`), three critical visual discrepancies were identified relative to the legacy `renderStoryVideo` engine:
1. **Missing 3D Depth Cutouts in IR Rendering**: When rendering from relative paths (e.g. `sample-story/story-config.json`), asset paths in `StoryConfigToUniversalIRAdapter` remained relative (`assets/scene1.jpg`). As a result, `getStoryRootAndCacheDir` could not find `story-config.json` and failed to locate pre-generated cutouts in `autogenerated/depth-3d/` or find the source image on disk. This caused the renderer to fall back to an elliptical mask instead of the true cutout.
2. **Black Gaps in Scene Transitions**: In `StoryConfigToUniversalIRAdapter`, scene video sequences were scheduled via audio anchor duration (`{ matchLengthOf: audioSeqId }`) without overlapping with the outgoing sequence during transitions. For crossfade transitions, the incoming video sequence must start at `outgoing.endFrame - transitionDurationFrames` (using `rs.after` with `overlapFrames: transitionFrames`). Without `overlapFrames`, the outgoing scene ended before the incoming scene started, leaving a visual cut or dip to black rather than a true overlapping crossfade.
3. **Parallax and Sandwich Particle Parity**: In `packages/ir/src/plugins/vfx/Depth3DPlugin.ts`, particle systems (`particles` and `dustParticles`) were not cached or passed into `renderer.renderFrame(...)`, leading to static layers rather than animated floating particles.
4. **Auto-Discovery of CWD Story Root**: In `getStoryRootAndCacheDir`, ancestor directory traversal was limited to 3 levels and did not fall back to `process.cwd()`.

---

## 2. Technical Implementation

### A. BaseDir & Relative Path Resolution in `StoryConfigToUniversalIRAdapter`
- Updated `StoryConfigToUniversalIRAdapter`:
  - `adapt()` accepts `options.baseDir` and resolves all asset paths (`rawVisualSrc`, `rawAudioSrc`, `colorGrading.lutPath`, `clip.source`, `settings.bgm.source`) to absolute paths via `resolvePath()`.
  - In `IRRenderCommand.ts`, passed `{ baseDir }` to `registry.transform(parsed, { baseDir })`.

### B. Transition Overlap Timing in Two-Pass Relational DAG
- Updated video sequence timing in `StoryConfigToUniversalIRAdapter`:
  - Added `overlapFrames: (index > 0 && transitionDurationFrames > 0) ? transitionDurationFrames : 0` to `videoSequences[i].time.start`:
    - `start: { after: seq_${index}_vis, overlapFrames }`
    - `duration: { matchLengthOf: audioSeqId, paddingFrames: pauseFrames + overlapFrames }`
  - This ensures the incoming scene starts exactly `overlapFrames` before the outgoing scene terminates, allowing `TransitionCrossfadePlugin` to smoothly blend incoming scenes over outgoing scenes with zero black frames.

### C. Particle Systems & Mesh Layer Caches in `Depth3DPlugin`
- Pre-allocated and cached:
  - `particles` (28 floating particles for `parallax_25d` / `parallax_dolly`)
  - `dustParticles` (35 ambient dust particles for `volumetric_sandwich` / `sandwich`)
  - `baseCanvas` (pre-rendered canvas for `wigglegram`)
- Forwarded these pre-allocated structures into `Depth3DFactory.getRenderer(mode).renderFrame(...)` while strictly adhering to `ZERO-ALLOC-FRAME-LOOP`.

### D. Robust Story Root Discovery in `getStoryRootAndCacheDir`
- Added ancestor searching up to 5 levels and fallback to `process.cwd()`.
