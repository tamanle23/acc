# History Log 148: Universal Video IR Engine (Consolidation into packages/ir)

- **Task Title:** Universal Video IR Engine: Consolidate Phases 1 through 6 into `packages/ir`
- **Task Scope:** `packages/ir`, `packages/core`, `packages/effect-engines`, `apps/cli`, `autogenerated-tests/`, `docs/history/`
- **Date Completed:** 2026-10-09
- **Status:** Verified & Tested (100% Pass across all 6 Phases)

---

## 1. Problem Statement
To strictly isolate legacy media pipelines (`packages/core`, `packages/effect-engines`) from the new Universal Video IR declarative engine, all implementations developed across Phases 1 through 6 needed to be centralized and encapsulated within `packages/ir`. This cleanly segregates new IR subsystems from existing imperative pipelines and simplifies maintenance, packaging, and zero-regression testing.

---

## 2. Plan of Action
1. **Directory Structure & Relocation to `packages/ir`:**
   - Move Command implementations: `RenderCommand.ts` -> `packages/ir/src/commands/`.
   - Move Modifier Plugin Registry & standard bridges (Depth3D, DynamicDucking, SplitEdit, TransitionCrossfade, BlendMode) -> `packages/ir/src/plugins/`.
   - Move Deterministic Rendering & Preview engines (DeterministicNoiseGenerator, FrameBackpressureLock, DecoupledAudioMixer, IRPreviewEngine, IRMasterRenderer) -> `packages/ir/src/renderer/`.
2. **Package Isolation & Cleanup:**
   - Remove IR renderer from `packages/core/src/renderer/ir/` and remove export from `packages/core/src/index.ts`.
   - Remove plugins and RenderCommand from `packages/effect-engines/` and clean up `packages/effect-engines/src/index.ts`.
   - Update `packages/ir/src/index.ts` to export all commands, plugins, and renderer components.
3. **Consumer & Test Alignment:**
   - Update `apps/cli/src/commands/ir-render.ts` and `StoryOrchestratedRenderStage.ts` to import `IRMasterRenderer` directly from `@acc/ir`.
   - Update all formal test suites in `autogenerated-tests/` (`video_ir_phase3_plugins`, `video_ir_phase4_engine`, `video_ir_phase5_parity`, `video_ir_phase6_cli_scaling`) to import solely from `@acc/ir`.
4. **Verification:**
   - Run all 6 Video IR test suites to ensure 100% pass without side effects.
