## 1. Context & Motivation
Following Phase 1 (`MediaAssetProberChain`) and Phase 2 (`PipelineClassifierRegistry`), `auto-config` possessed structured directory manifests and heuristic pipeline archetypes. However, it lacked the synthesis engines required to construct validated `PipelineIR` (v3.0.0) documents and polymorphic `FineGrainedVideoDirectorConfig` envelopes.

## 2. Technical Implementation
1. **`PipelineIRBuilder` (GoF Builder Pattern) in `packages/core/src/autoconfig/builders/PipelineIRBuilder.ts`**:
   - Implements a fluent, progressive construction API with strict invariant validation on `.build()`.
   - Autonomous pairing and chunking logic via `.populateFromManifest(manifest, archetypeDefaults)`:
     - Handles pair-indexed files (`manifest.pairedPairs`), chronological media series, or text script lines for scraper pipelines.
     - Auto-configures BGM stems, audio ducking (-14dB), voiceover loudness (-14 LUFS), and distribution exporter hints.
     - Enforces chunk ordering, positive duration minimums, and immutable output objects.
2. **`DirectorPresetFactory` (GoF Factory Pattern) in `packages/core/src/autoconfig/factories/DirectorPresetFactory.ts`**:
   - Centralizes generation of production-grade `FineGrainedVideoDirectorConfig` envelopes across all 3 cinematography tiers:
     - **Pipeline 1 (Scraper & Summarizer)**: Clean tech LUT (`clean_tech`), minimal editorial typography, gentle Ken Burns zoom-in hook, and clean crossfades.
     - **Pipeline 2 (DB-Templating)**: 60fps high framerate, bold headline typography, `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` chart animation curves, and impact bass SFX.
     - **Pipeline 3 (Repurposing Recycler)**: 9:16 vertical canvas, viral TikTok karaoke glow, talking head auto-reframe (`center_face`), emphasis punch-in, and whoosh SFX.
     - **Pipeline 4 (Generative Storytelling)**: 3D parallax / frame-breaker sandwich typography, cinematic teal & orange LUT (`cinematic_teal_orange`), crossfades, and ambient dust particles.
     - **Pipeline 5 (Rule-Logic)**: Cyberpunk neon LUT, glitch transitions, and notification audio cues.
     - **Pipeline 6 (Ambient Loops)**: Warm golden LUT (`warm_golden`), prominent uninterrupted BGM (volume 0.85, autoDucking false), and long 1200ms crossfades.
   - Respects user configuration options (`aspectRatio`, `fps`, `enableDepth3D`, `enableTransitions`).
3. **Exports & Types**:
