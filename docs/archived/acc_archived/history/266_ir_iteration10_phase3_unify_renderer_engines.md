# History Entry 266: IR Iteration 10 Phase 3 - Unify IRMasterRenderer & IRPreviewEngine

## Context & Objective
In Iteration 10, the goal is 100% bit-identical visual parity between client/browser (`IRPreviewEngine`) and server/headless (`IRMasterRenderer`).
Phase 1 unified the plugin ecosystem (`TransformModifierPlugin`, `CaptionModifierPlugin`), and Phase 2 introduced the canonical `IRCanvasCompositor` with pure document-order track composition and Hybrid Pragmatic Culling.

Phase 3 targets the elimination of divergent rendering loops in `IRMasterRenderer` and `IRPreviewEngine` by delegating their canvas operations directly to `IRCanvasCompositor.renderFrame()`.

## Changes Executed
1. **`IRMasterRenderer.ts`**:
   - Refactored `renderCanvasFrame()` to asynchronously pre-fetch video frames from active decoders into an `assetResolver` (`IAssetSourceResolver`).
   - Replaced internal manual track sorting, blend modes, motion evaluation, and separate caption rendering with a single call to `IRCanvasCompositor.renderFrame()`.
   - Set default viewport clear color to `#000000` (Pure Black).
   - Removed redundant internal caption strategy properties and duplicate track iteration logic.
2. **`IRPreviewEngine.ts`**:
   - Updated `PreviewRenderOptions` to support `clearColor?: string`.
   - Replaced ~180 lines of duplicated track sorting, blend mode application, motion transformations, clip-space plugins, texture blitting, stream-space modifiers, and master composite passes with a direct delegation to `IRCanvasCompositor.renderFrame()`.
   - Defaulted viewport clear color to `#000000`.
3. **`packages/ir/src/renderer/index.ts`**:
   - Exported `IRPreviewEngine`, `AssetTextureCache`, and `DeterministicMotionEvaluator` to ensure consistent imports across `@acc/ir` and `@acc/ir/client`.
