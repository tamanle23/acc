# Walkthrough 03: Enterprise-Grade Studio Dual-Sidebar Workspace, @dnd-kit Staging & Deep Engine Wiring

## 1. Overview of Delivered Systems
We upgraded `apps/studio` into a digital workstation featuring:
1. **Enterprise Dual-Sidebar Architecture**:
   - **Far-Left 48px Activity Rail + 300px Slide-out Drawer (`LeftSidebar.tsx`)**:
     - Dedicated modes for **Staging Library** (12 categorized presets across Text, Vector Shapes, and Partial VFX) and **Assets Vault** (type filters for Video, Audio, Image).
     - Collapse and expand trigger with smooth transition and `[` hotkey.
   - **Right Dedicated Property Inspector (336px) (`RightSidebar.tsx`)**:
     - Active Sequence Inspector with NLE Action Bar (Duplicate Sequence, Delete Sequence with 100% undo recovery, Lock/Bypass toggle, and ID copy).
     - Add Modifier dropdown with checkmark indicators.
     - Deep collapsible accordions for Transform, 3D Relighting, 3D Camera, Particle VFX, Typography, and Audio Ducking.
     - Fallback State (when no sequence is selected): Project & Canvas Settings (Resolution, FPS, Color Space, Master Duration, Active Tracks count).
2. **`@dnd-kit` Staging Pipeline**:
   - Replaced HTML5 drag-and-drop with pure `@dnd-kit/core` and `@dnd-kit/utilities`.
   - `PointerSensor` configured with `activationConstraint: { distance: 6 }` to cleanly isolate click actions (button presses, text selection) from drag gestures.
   - Hardware-accelerated `<DragOverlay>` rendering crisp preview cards without OS ghost image glitches.
   - `useDroppable` registration on the canvas viewport (`canvas-viewport-drop-zone`) with neon cyan drop aura.
3. **Deep Engine Wiring (`UniversalThreeScene` & `UniversalAudioMixer`)**:
   - **`UniversalThreeScene`**: Single Three.js `WebGLRenderer` bound directly to the viewport canvas. Integrates 3D depth relief mesh, holographic HUD plane, 1500-particle warp tunnel, partial VFX regions (spotlight, blur, glitch, spark emitters), and in-canvas vector transform overlay into one depth-tested scenegraph.
   - **`UniversalAudioMixer`**: Dynamically syncs `sfx/dynamic_ducking` modifiers (`duckVolume`, `attackMs`, `releaseMs`, `stereoPan`) from `IRStateManager` into the audio mixer, synthesizing stereo Float32 PCM buffers and streaming live VU levels to `MasterAudioMeter.tsx`.
4. **Workspace Velocity & Zen Mode**:
   - Titlebar toggles for Left Drawer and Right Inspector.
   - Keyboard shortcuts: `[` (left drawer toggle), `]` (right inspector toggle), `\` (Zen / full canvas focus mode).
