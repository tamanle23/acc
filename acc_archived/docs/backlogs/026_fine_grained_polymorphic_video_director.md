## Context & Problem
In `story-config.json`, authors have fine-grained scene-level director control over 3D depth (`mode`, `parallaxFactor`, `focusDepth`, `sandwich`), camera motion (`pan`, `zoom`), transitions (`xfade`, `wipe`), color grading, and SFX cue timing. However, `story-config.json` is statically tied to manually curated slide/scene image arrays and does not support dynamic programmatic fan-out across 250 disparate content variations (crypto recaps, bar chart races, podcast shorts, educational explainers, bedtime stories).

Previously, `videoConfig` in `ModalityConfigurations` only exposed rudimentary top-level metadata (`aspectRatio`, `fps`, `durationMs`). There was no unified mechanism to direct per-segment cinematics, 3D typography sandwiching, camera motion curves, SFX cues, or domain-specific visual graphics while maintaining polymorphism across diverse content types.

---

## Proposed Design
Implemented a **3-Tier Hierarchical & Polymorphic Video Director Architecture**:
1. **Tier 1: Global Director Directives**: Canvas dimensions, FPS, resolution scaling, master color grading LUT, and master BGM ducking envelope.
2. **Tier 2: Content-Type Specific Archetype Directives**: Polymorphic visual knobs for domain specializations (e.g. `chartVisuals`, `cryptoVisuals`, `talkingHead`).
3. **Tier 3: Timeline & Segment Director Overrides**:
   - **Semantic Role Directives**: Directing by narrative role (`hook`, `body`, `outro`).
   - **Explicit Segment Directives**: Fine-grained per-segment overrides for `depth3d`, `cameraMotion`, `transition`, `captions`, and `sfxCues`.

Compiler Integration:
- Enhanced `PipelineIRToUniversalIRAdapter.adapt` to resolve the 3 tiers into canonical `VideoIRDocument` sequences, tracks, and `ModifierNode[]` (`vfx/depth3d`, `transform/ken_burns`, `transition/crossfade`, `vfx/color_grading`, timed SFX tracks, and timed text tracks).
- Integrated into `BaseContentTaskStrategy.renderVideo`.
- Guaranteed 100% backward compatibility for existing lightweight configs.
