## 1. Context & Problem
In our omnichannel generation architecture, Pipelines 1 (`01-scraper`), 2 (`02-db-templating`), and 3 (`03-repurposing`) produce diverse multi-modal assets across formats:
- **Video outputs** (Alert videos, animated bar charts, 9:16 vertical shorts) require high-performance, deterministic multi-core rendering.
- **Non-video outputs** (podcasts/audio-only narration, static infographics PNG/SVG, social text/captions/markdown alerts) should **never** incur video rasterization overhead or construct unnecessary `VideoIRDocument` frame rendering loops.

**Strict Architectural Constraint (Phase 4 / Option 3)**:
Only pipelines with **video output** compile directly into the Universal Video IR (`VideoIRDocument`); non-video output artifacts (audio-only podcasts, static infographics/PNG summaries, markdown/text alerts) bypass the Video IR engine completely and emit directly via dedicated audio/image pipelines.

---

## 2. Proposed Design
1. **PipelineIRToUniversalIRAdapter (`packages/ir/src/adapters/PipelineIRToUniversalIRAdapter.ts`)**:
   - Adapts `PipelineIR` (from Pipelines 1, 2, 3) into canonical `VideoIRDocument` when a video target is requested.
   - Registers into `IRConfigAdapterRegistry`.
2. **Output-Modality Selective Dispatcher in `VideoExporter`**:
   - In `packages/exporters/src/video/VideoExporter.ts`, route video generation through `IRMasterRenderer` and `FFIVideoEncoderSink` (or `IRChunkWorkerPool`), eliminating legacy ad-hoc canvas encoders.
   - Non-video exporters (`AudioExporter`, `StaticWebExporter`, `SocialExporter`) execute pure native audio slicing, image encoding, and text writing without touching the Video IR engine.
3. **Omnichannel Pipelines 1, 2, 3 Verification**:
   - Pipeline 1 (Event Scraper): Ingests event, synthesizes audio & card, routes video path into Universal Video IR and audio/text directly to disk.
   - Pipeline 2 (DB Templating): Ingests structured metrics, generates bar charts, compiles video path to Universal Video IR and static assets to PNG/JSON.
   - Pipeline 3 (Repurposing): Extracts viral hooks & transcript timestamps, compiles video path to Universal Video IR and podcast audio/subtitles directly.
