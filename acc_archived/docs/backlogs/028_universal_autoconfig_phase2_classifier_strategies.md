## Context & Problem
In Phase 1, we implemented the headless `MediaAssetProberChain`, generating a comprehensive `DiscoveredAssetManifest`. However, `auto-config` still lacks the intelligence to automatically deduce which of the 6 Canonical Pipelines (Scraper/Summarizer, DB-Templating, Repurposing, Storytelling, Rule-Logic, Ambient Loops) best fits the discovered media signatures.

We need an extensible, Open-Closed **Strategy Pattern** and a central **`PipelineClassifierRegistry`** that scores manifest patterns, suggests default modalities, and resolves the optimal pipeline archetype with confidence metrics.

---

## Proposed Design
Implement **Phase 2 of Universal Auto-Config**:
1. **Contract Definition (`IPipelineClassifierStrategy`)**:
   - `calculateConfidence(manifest)` returning normalized score `[0.0, 1.0]`.
   - `generateArchetypeDefaults(manifest)` providing pipeline-specific defaults, suggested modalities, and timing rules.
2. **6 Concrete Classifier Strategies**:
   - `ScraperSummarizerClassifierStrategy` (Pipeline 1): Script/Text-heavy folders.
   - `DbTemplatingClassifierStrategy` (Pipeline 2): CSV/JSON datasets.
   - `RepurposingClassifierStrategy` (Pipeline 3): Pre-existing long-form videos.
   - `GenerativeStoryClassifierStrategy` (Pipeline 4): Sequential images + voiceovers.
   - `RuleLogicClassifierStrategy` (Pipeline 5): Branching logic and rule definitions.
   - `AmbientLoopClassifierStrategy` (Pipeline 6): Looping music/ambient stems with textures.
3. **Central Registry (`PipelineClassifierRegistry`)**:
   - Automatic registration of all 6 strategies.
   - Intelligent heuristic resolution with confidence ranking and manual override support.
   - Safe fallback to Pipeline 4 when input signals are ambiguous.
