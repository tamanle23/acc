## 🎯 Executive Summary
In Iteration 9 Phase 4, we completed the architectural unification of the Studio preview subsystem by decommissioning the legacy monolithic `UniversalCanvasEngine.ts`. In its place, interactive preview and headless client export now share the official `IRPreviewEngine` and modular plugin architecture (`CanvasParticleOverlayPlugin`, `HologramHUDPlugin`, `PartialVFXPlugin`, and `DeterministicMotionEvaluator`). All 7 affected unit test suites in `autogenerated-tests/` were migrated to test the new components directly without losing a single assertion.

## 🛠️ Changes Implemented

### 1. Decommissioning & Cleanup (`packages/effect-engines`)
- **`packages/effect-engines/src/canvas/UniversalCanvasEngine.ts`:**
  - Completely deleted the monolithic file.
- **`packages/effect-engines/src/index.ts` & `packages/effect-engines/src/client.ts`:**
  - Removed `UniversalCanvasEngine` and `UniversalThreeScene` exports.
  - Retained all vector transform tools: `CanvasTransformOverlay`, `MagneticSnapEngine`, `UniversalCanvasProvider`, `CanvasAssetLoader`, and associated types.

### 2. Test Suite Migration (`autogenerated-tests`)
- **`autogenerated-tests/isomorphic_canvas_engine.test.ts`:**
  - Migrated from testing `UniversalCanvasEngine` to testing `IRPreviewEngine`, `CanvasTransformOverlay`, `HologramHUDPlugin`, and `PartialVFXPlugin` directly across Node/Canvas2D contexts.
- **`autogenerated-tests/studio_canvas_transform.test.ts`:**
  - Removed `UniversalCanvasEngine as UniversalThreeScene` dependency; verified standalone lifecycle and zero memory leaks in `CanvasTransformOverlay`.
- **`autogenerated-tests/studio_phase1_declarative_vfx.test.ts`:**
  - Replaced ad-hoc particle/HUD engine assertions with declarative validation and target scope tests on `CanvasParticleOverlayPlugin` and `HologramHUDPlugin`.
- **`autogenerated-tests/studio_phase4_inspector_hud_particles.test.ts`:**
  - Migrated Section 4 to test `CanvasParticleOverlayPlugin` and `HologramHUDPlugin` properties and scope reactivity directly.
- **`autogenerated-tests/studio_phase6_motion_timeline.test.ts`:**
  - Migrated Section 1 from `UniversalCanvasEngine.syncTimeline` to `evaluateSequenceMotion` and `evaluateEase` in `DeterministicMotionEvaluator`.
- **`autogenerated-tests/studio_phase7_master_e2e_hardening.test.ts`:**
  - Migrated Pillar 1 (Strict Bit-Identical Byte Hashing Parity) to render multi-track timelines via `IRPreviewEngine` on `NodeCanvasAdapter`, validating 100% SHA-256 byte parity.
  - Migrated Pillar 2 (High-Density 60fps Benchmark & Heap Stability) to evaluate `IRPreviewEngine` frame rendering and seek stability.
  - Migrated Pillar 3 NaN and non-positive transform sanitization to `evaluateSequenceMotion`.
- **`autogenerated-tests/studio_three_audio_wiring.test.ts` & `studio_iteration6_e2e.test.ts` & `studio_partial_vfx_staging.test.ts`:**
  - Removed leftover `UniversalThreeScene` imports and migrated tests to `PartialVFXPlugin` and `CanvasTransformOverlay`.
