# [163] Fix IR Transition Gap Black Frames & Native B-Roll Video Decoding

- **Status:** `[x] Completed`
- **Date:** 2026-10-10
- **Scope:** `packages/core/src/ffmpeg-ffi/`, `packages/ir/src/adapters/`, `packages/ir/src/plugins/editorial/`, `packages/ir/src/renderer/`

---

## 1. Context & Problem Statement
During video playback comparisons between `renderStoryVideo` and `ir-render`, two critical discrepancies were observed:
1. **Black screen interval between scenes during transitions / pauses**:
   - `StoryConfigToUniversalIRAdapter` mapped visual sequence duration strictly to voiceover duration (`matchLengthOf: audioSeqId`), but voiceovers ended before the `pauseAfter` interval. This left an uncovered time gap where no visual sequence was active, causing the canvas to fill with black (`#000000`).
   - `TransitionCrossfadePlugin` was fading down to black at the tail of sequences (`localFrame > totalSeqFrames - dur`), compounding the dip-to-black effect instead of smoothly blending into the next scene.
2. **B-Roll video scenes not rendering**:
   - `IRMasterRenderer.preloadAssets` only checked for static image extensions (`.jpg`, `.png`, etc.). When a scene used a video asset (`.mp4`, `.mov`, etc.), it was skipped, leaving `baseAsset` undefined and resulting in a blank screen.

---

## 2. Changes Made
1. **Native In-Memory Video Decoder (`packages/core/src/ffmpeg-ffi/FFIVideoDecoder.ts`)**:
   - Implemented `FFIVideoDecoder` leveraging `libavcodec`, `libavformat`, and `libswscale` via pure `bun:ffi`.
   - Provides asynchronous `readNextFrame(): Promise<Canvas | null>` with automatic looping and letterbox/pillarbox canvas generation.
   - Exported in `packages/core/src/index.ts`.
2. **B-Roll Asset Handling in `IRMasterRenderer` (`packages/ir/src/renderer/IRMasterRenderer.ts`)**:
   - In `preloadAssets`, dynamically initializes and caches `FFIVideoDecoder` for video assets (`.mp4`, `.mov`, `.mkv`, `.webm`, `.avi`).
   - In `renderCanvasFrame`, retrieves decoded frames from `videoDecoders` and draws them to the master canvas with aspect-ratio preservation.
   - Properly closes and releases all native decoder contexts upon render finalization.
3. **Continuous Visual Timeline Mapping in `StoryConfigToUniversalIRAdapter`**:
   - Extended video sequence duration with `paddingFrames: pauseFrames` so visual scenes stay active through pauses and transitions, eliminating gaps.
4. **Dip-to-Black Prevention in `TransitionCrossfadePlugin`**:
   - Modified crossfade behavior so incoming sequences fade in over the existing active frame without outgoing scenes fading out to black.
