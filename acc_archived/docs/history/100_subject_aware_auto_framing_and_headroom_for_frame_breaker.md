## 1. Context & Problem Statement
In `sample-story` Scene 1, the character (dinosaur) was not visibly breaking out of the top card frame because:
1. The card frame was vertically centered without headroom, causing the top border to sit too close to the canvas top ($y \approx 97\text{px}$).
2. The inner crop parameter $sy$ was calculated from a generic small offset rather than the character's anatomical neck/shoulder line, leaving the head located below the top border of the card frame.

---

## 2. Solution & Architectural Implementation

### Subject Bounding Box-Aware Framing & Pop-Out Headroom
1. **Dynamic Subject Bounding Box Detection**:
   - Analyzed `subjectImg` non-transparent alpha mask to locate $(minX, maxX, minY, maxY)$.
   - Calculated target crop cut line at the anatomical neck/chest:
     ```typescript
     const subCutY = subBBox.minY + (subBBox.maxY - subBBox.minY) * 0.30;
     ```
2. **Subject-Centered Framing**:
   - `sw = img.width / innerZoom`, `sh = img.height / innerZoom`.
   - `sx` aligns horizontally with the character's centroid: `(subBBox.minX + subBBox.maxX) / 2 - sw / 2`.
   - `sy` aligns vertically with the neck line: `Math.max(0, Math.min(img.height - sh, subCutY))`.
3. **Card Dimension & Headroom Alignment**:
   - `targetInnerH = bgH * 0.52` with card frame positioned at `(height - frameH) / 2 + bgH * 0.05 + floatY`.
   - Leaves ample headroom (>90px) above the card frame.
   - Pop-out distance: head $(y = 171\text{px})$ projects **~92 pixels clearly above the top border of the card frame** into the ambient backdrop plate.
