# 249 - Studio Phase 3: Platform-Independent Audio Core, Pure Plugins & Cache

## Overview
Phase 3 establishes a platform-independent audio architecture within `packages/ir`, decouples audio DSP from visual effects engines, registers a complete suite of pure Float32 buffer audio modifier plugins, and implements reactive asynchronous audio asset decoding in Apps/Studio with real-time VU analysis and waveform visualization.

## Problem Solved
1. **Misplaced Audio Core**: `UniversalAudioMixer` was previously located in `packages/effect-engines`, creating an inverted dependency where audio mixing was coupled with visual effect rendering rather than the core intermediate representation.
2. **Hardcoded Procedural-Only Audio**: Timeline playback synthesized generic tones and laser SFX rather than decoding and mixing real audio assets from URLs.
3. **Absence of Audio Modifier Plugins**: Volume, panning, frequency filtering, and speed adjustments were either hardcoded or missing from the modifier plugin registry.
4. **Playback Latency on Audio Buffering**: There was no reactive audio cache with an Observer pattern to notify the timeline when audio assets finished fetching and decoding.

## Solution Architecture
1. **Platform-Independent Audio Core (`packages/ir/src/renderer/audio/`)**:
   - Relocated `UniversalAudioMixer.ts` to `packages/ir/src/renderer/audio/` alongside `DecoupledAudioMixer.ts` and `DynamicAudioDuckingEngine.ts`.
   - Exported pure browser client entrypoints via `packages/ir/src/client.ts` guaranteeing zero native C or `@napi-rs/canvas` dependencies.
   - Maintained backward-compatibility bridge in `packages/effect-engines/src/audio/UniversalAudioMixer.ts` re-exporting from `@acc/ir/client`.
2. **Audio Source Adapter Pattern (`AudioSourceAdapter.ts`)**:
   - `IAudioSourceProvider`: Clean interface for sampling time slices at target sample rates.
   - `DecodedPcmSource`: Samples from real decoded Float32Array PCM channel buffers, performing linear resampling when source sample rate differs from mixer rate.
   - `ProceduralSynthesizerSource`: Deterministic DSP fallback when assets are buffering or offline.
3. **Pure Buffer-Based Modifier Plugins (`packages/ir/src/plugins/audio/` & `sfx/`)**:
   - `VolumeModifierPlugin` (`audio/volume`): Linear gain scaling and cosine/linear fade-in and fade-out curves.
   - `PanModifierPlugin` (`audio/pan`): Equal-power stereo panning conserving energy across left/right channels.
   - `FilterModifierPlugin` (`audio/filter`): 2-pole Biquad IIR filter (lowpass, highpass, bandpass) with frequency cutoff and resonance Q.
   - `PitchModifierPlugin` (`audio/pitch`): Linear interpolation resampling for playback speed and cents detune.
   - `DynamicDuckingPlugin` (`sfx/dynamic_ducking`): Sidechain compression envelope calculation.
4. **Master Bus Architecture in `UniversalAudioMixer`**:
   - **Clip Level**: Executes modifier plugins sequentially on raw channel buffers.
   - **Track Buses**: Sub-mixes clips into dedicated Voice, SFX, and BGM buses.
   - **Master Bus**: Uses `DynamicAudioDuckingEngine` to generate continuous attack/hold/release sidechain gain envelopes to duck BGM during voiceover, followed by a soft-clipping master limiter (`Math.tanh`).
5. **Studio Audio Asset Cache (`apps/studio/src/lib/audio/AudioAssetCache.ts`)**:
   - Observer pattern (`subscribe(callback)`, `notify()`) mirroring `AssetTextureCache`.
   - Asynchronously decodes audio URLs via browser `AudioContext.decodeAudioData()`, caching Float32Array channels.
6. **WebAudioEngine Integration (`apps/studio/src/lib/audio/WebAudioEngine.ts`)**:
   - Subscribes to `AudioAssetCache` to automatically re-sync decoded audio into `UniversalAudioMixer`.
   - Extracts real PCM waveform peak profiles for visual timeline display.
