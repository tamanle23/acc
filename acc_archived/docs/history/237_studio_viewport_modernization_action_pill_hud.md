# 237. Studio Viewport Modernization (Canvas2D, Action Pill, Viewport HUD)

## Context & Problem
In **Iteration 7 (Phase 4)**, the studio preview viewport needed to evolve beyond a simple video frame monitor into an enterprise-grade NLE canvas viewport with:
1. **Interactive Viewport Header HUD & Navigation**:
   - Quick Zoom presets (`Fit`, `50%`, `100%`, `200%`) allowing editors to scale into detailed pixel inspection or overview frames.
   - Pan Hand mode and Spacebar-drag navigation when zoomed in.
   - Dual-mode broadcast & social safe guidelines (16:9 Action/Title Safe and 9:16 vertical Reels/TikTok safe bounds with danger zones).
   - Alpha transparency checkerboard pattern behind transparent video, title graphics, and vector shapes.
2. **On-Canvas Floating Contextual Action Pill**:
   - Direct contextual manipulation pill attached to the active selection bounding box with quick access to Duplicate, Center Alignment, Lock/Unlock transform, Reset transform, and Delete.
3. **Responsive Cursor Feedback & Hotkeys**:
   - Contextual cursors for resize handles (`nwse-resize`, `nesw-resize`, `ns-resize`, `ew-resize`), rotation knob (`cursor-grab`/`cursor-grabbing`), pan tool, and locked state (`cursor-not-allowed`).
   - Global keyboard shortcuts (`Space` for Pan/Playhead, `Ctrl+D` for Duplicate, `Delete`/`Backspace` for Delete, `Esc` for Deselect).

## Implementation Details

1. **Viewport Header HUD & Controls (`apps/studio/src/components/Preview.tsx`)**:
   - Integrated top right viewport HUD:
     - **Zoom Presets Segmented Pill**: `Fit`, `50%`, `100%`, `200%` with instantaneous scale updates.
     - **Pan Tool Button**: Toggle button activating `isPanToolActive` and holding `Space` enabling interactive viewport panning.
     - **Safe Guides Cycle Button**: Toggles between `Off`, `16:9 Broadcast Safe` (Action Safe 93% and Title Safe 90% with center crosshair), and `9:16 Social Safe` (Reels/TikTok frame with Top UI danger zone 12%, Bottom captions danger zone 22%, and Right action buttons danger zone 22%).
     - **Alpha Checkerboard Button**: Toggles `repeating-conic-gradient` alpha background and passes through canvas clear pass.

2. **On-Canvas Floating Contextual Action Pill**:
   - Attached at `-top-12 left-1/2 -translate-x-1/2` relative to the active sequence's bounding box.
   - Rendered with liquid glass styling (`glass-panel`, `shadow-2xl`, `backdrop-blur-xl`).
   - Wired actions:
     - **Duplicate (`Copy`)**: Dispatches `DuplicateSequenceCommand` through `IRStateManager.getInstance().duplicateSequence(selectedSeqId)` and focuses the new copy.
     - **Center Alignment (`AlignCenter`)**: Centers `x: 0, y: 0` coordinates in the transform modifier.
     - **Lock / Unlock (`Lock` / `Unlock`)**: Toggles `isLocked`, disabling handle dragging and switching handles to amber `cursor-not-allowed` styles.
     - **Reset Transform (`RotateCcw`)**: Resets scale to 1.0, rotation to 0, and offset to 0.
     - **Delete (`Trash2`)**: Dispatches `DeleteSequenceCommand` through `IRStateManager.getInstance().deleteSequence(selectedSeqId)`.

3. **Responsive Cursor States & Global Shortcuts**:
   - Handle cursor states: `cursor-nwse-resize`, `cursor-nesw-resize`, `cursor-ns-resize`, `cursor-ew-resize`, and `cursor-grab`.
   - Global keyboard listeners for `Space`, `Delete`, `Backspace`, `Ctrl+D`, and `Escape` with guards against active text inputs.
   - Global `window.mouseup` listener ensuring panning and dragging release reliably even if the mouse leaves the browser frame.
