# History Log 118: Synchronize All Story Scenes, Voiceovers, and Captions

- **Date:** 2026-10-02
- **Author:** Antigravity Principal Engineer
- **Component:** `sample-story` (`story-config.json`), `@acc/cli` (`StoryRenderer`, `tts`, `whisper`)

---

## 1. Problem Statement & Root Cause Analysis

During story video playback, voiceover, caption, and visual scenes were severely mismatched:
1. **Scenes 13 to 28 Audio Re-use**: Scenes 13 through 28 had specific descriptive scripts for different 3D depth engines (e.g. "Bục 3D Isometric Podium...", "Cổng không gian Cutout Portal...", "Băng chuyền 3D...", "Lưới không gian..."), but their `audio` paths were erroneously pointing to `assets/voiceover1.mp3` ("Ngày xửa ngày xưa...") and `assets/voiceover2.mp3` ("Ở một vương quốc xa xôi..."), causing wrong spoken audio across 16 scenes.
2. **Artificial Timing Compression in Captions**: Scenes 3, 6, 7, 8 had dummy spans `[0..2.0s]` for 14-17 words, forcing words to flash in 0.1s while TTS audio was still speaking.
3. **Scenes 13-28 Single-Word Fallback**: Captions in scenes 13-28 were single 1-element arrays rather than full word-by-word active highlight alignments.

---

## 2. Solution Plan & Implementation Details

1. **Synthesized Dedicated Voiceovers for All Scenes**:
   - Used Vieneu / IPCFacade Vietnamese TTS (`voice: "Ngọc Trân"`, `speed: 1.0`) to generate individual distinct voiceover files for scenes 3 through 28 matching their exact narration text.
   - Retained custom audio assets for Scene 1 (`voiceover1.mp3`) and Scene 2 (`voiceover2.mp3`).
2. **Whisper Script-Guided Word Alignment**:
   - Transcribed each synthesized audio track with `alignScriptWithTimestamps` to produce exact word-level start/end timestamps spanning the natural speech duration (2.4s to 6.4s).
3. **Synchronized Story Configuration**:
   - Updated [`sample-story/story-config.json`](../../sample-story/story-config.json) with accurate `audio`, `text`, and `captions` across all 28 scenes.
