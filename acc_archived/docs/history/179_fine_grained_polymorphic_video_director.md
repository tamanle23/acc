# [179] Fine-Grained Polymorphic Video Director Control for Content Strategies

## 1. Overview & Context
Prior to this task, `story-config.json` provided fine-grained scene-level director control (3D depth, Ken Burns motions, transition types, and typography styling), but was tightly bound to static slide configurations. Conversely, dynamic content task strategies across 250 content variations were only equipped with coarse video metadata (`aspectRatio`, `fps`, `durationMs`).

This task implements a **3-Tier Hierarchical & Polymorphic Video Director Architecture**, allowing callers to specify rich directorial cinematics, audio staging, and visual overlays on any omnichannel content strategy while preserving full polymorphism across diverse domains (crypto alerts, bar chart races, podcast clips, and generative narratives).

---

## 2. Architecture & Design

### Tier 1: Global Director Canvas & Atmosphere
- **Canvas & Scaling**: Aspect ratio ('9:16', '16:9', '1:1', '4:5') and resolution presets ('720p', '1080p', '4k') mapped dynamically to native pixel dimensions.
- **Master Color Grading**: LUT presets (`cyberpunk_neon`, `cinematic_teal_orange`, `clean_tech`), contrast, saturation, and vignette parameters compiled directly into `vfx/color_grading` track modifiers.
- **Audio Staging**: Master BGM track (`track_a2_bgm`) with ducking envelope (`autoDucking`, `duckingAttenuationDb`, `volume`).

### Tier 2: Domain Archetype Directives
- Specialized visual directives tailored for distinct content archetypes:
  - `chartVisuals`: Animation curve, leader zoom, glow.
  - `cryptoVisuals`: Ticker style (floating HUD, bottom tape), sentiment color coding.
  - `talkingHead`: Auto-reframe face tracking, punch-in cuts, audio waveform visualizer.
  - `narrativeStory`: Atmospheric fog, cinematic bars, ambient dust particles.

### Tier 3: Timeline & Segment Director Overrides
- **Semantic Role Directives**: Directing by narrative role (`hook`, `body`, `outro`) applied automatically to segment order.
- **Explicit Segment Directives**: Fine-grained per-segment overrides for:
  - `depth3d`: Parallax 2.5D, frame-breaker, and sandwich mode modifiers (`vfx/depth3d`).
  - `cameraMotion`: Ken Burns zoom in/out, pan, dolly zoom (`transform/ken_burns`).
  - `transition`: Crossfade, glitch, wipe with easing and frame-accurate overlap.
  - `sfxCues`: Timed SFX cue staging on dedicated audio track (`track_a3_sfx`) synced to visual sequences.
  - `captions` & `typography`: Timed text tracks (`track_t1_captions`) with customized typography presets (`3d_sandwich`, `tiktok_karaoke_glow`) and positioning.

## 3. Key Implementation Details

1. **`packages/ir/src/types/FineGrainedVideoDirectorConfig.ts`**:
   - Comprehensive TypeScript definitions for all 3 tiers.
   - Re-exported via `@acc/ir` and `@acc/core`.
2. **`packages/ir/src/adapters/PipelineIRToUniversalIRAdapter.ts`**:
   - Upgraded to V2.0.0.
   - Compiles global canvas, BGM, SFX cues, 3D depth, camera motion, and typography sequences into `VideoIRDocument`.
   - Resolves segment overrides cleanly with 1-based and 0-based collision avoidance.
3. **`packages/core/src/conductor/strategies/BaseContentTaskStrategy.ts`**:
   - `renderVideo` passes `FineGrainedVideoDirectorConfig` down to `PipelineIRToUniversalIRAdapter.adapt`.
   - Backward compatibility preserved for legacy simple configs.
4. **Defensive Coding & Hardening**:
   - Protected `Depth3DPlugin` with `fs.existsSync` and defensive `try/catch` to guarantee zero-crash execution when dealing with mock asset paths during unit testing.
