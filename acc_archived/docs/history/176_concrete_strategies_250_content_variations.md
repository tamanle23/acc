# History Entry [176]: Implement Concrete Content Task Strategies for All 250 Content Variations

## Context & Problem
While the ACC engine had full support for 6 Canonical Pipelines and 5 Modality Exporters, the actual catalog of 250 content types from `docs/4.250_CONTENT_TYPES_6_PIPELINES_INTEGRATION_MATRIX.md` lacked concrete strategy implementations. Only the abstract interface `IContentTaskStrategy` was defined, with 0 registered strategies in the runtime.

## Solution & Architecture
We implemented the **GoF Strategy + Registry + Template Method Pattern** across 6 phases:
1. **Core Strategy Framework (`@acc/core`)**:
   - `BaseContentTaskStrategy`: Abstract template method enforcing standard validation, metadata extraction, and `PipelineIR` creation.
   - `ContentStrategyRegistry`: Central thread-safe catalog indexing strategies by `contentTypeId`, `pipelineId`, and `targetModality`.
2. **5 Modality Archetypes**:
   - `StaticWebContentStrategy` (Format 1: Static Web CMS / SSG / Markdown)
   - `SocialMediaContentStrategy` (Format 2: Twitter threads, LinkedIn carousels, quote cards)
   - `VideoContentStrategy` (Format 3: Direct compilation into canonical `VideoIRDocument` Universal Video IR)
   - `AudioPodcastContentStrategy` (Format 4: EBU R128 podcast broadcast specs, bypassing Video IR)
   - `InteractiveWebContentStrategy` (Format 5: HTML5 widgets, D3 charts, games, calculators)
3. **All 250 Strategy Implementations Scaffolded**:
   - Items 1–50 (`Phase1StaticWebStrategies.ts`): Daily industry digests, tech specs, glossaries, recipe nutrition.
   - Items 51–100 (`Phase2SocialStrategies.ts`): Tweet threads, crypto fear & greed dials, WOD cards, breaking tickers.
   - Items 101–150 (`Phase3VideoStrategies.ts`): Faceless Reddit shorts, bar chart races, 3D depth reels, ambient loops.
   - Items 151–200 (`Phase4AudioStrategies.ts`): Solo podcasts, lo-fi beats, bedtime stories, binaural ASMR.
   - Items 201–250 (`Phase5InteractiveStrategies.ts`): Choose Your Own Adventure games, scrollytelling, ROI calculators, regex testers.
4. **Conductor Integration**:
   - Built `UniversalStrategyTaskCommand` (`acc.content.render_strategy`) and registered it in `TaskWorkerFactory`.
   - Built `registerAll250ContentStrategies()` to instantiate all 250 strategies at startup.
