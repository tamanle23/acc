## 1. Context & Objectives
In the Tri-Pillar Architecture Plan, Pillar 2 focuses on establishing production-grade native audio handling inside the Universal Video IR rendering pipeline:
1. **Universal Format Decoding:** DecoupledAudioMixer previously only decoded uncompressed WAV files. Any MP3, AAC, or M4A assets (such as `bgm.mp3` or Kokoro TTS outputs) were ignored or silent.
2. **Dynamic Broadcast-Grade Sidechain Ducking:** Replace static multiplier ducking with a continuous attack/hold/release sidechain gain envelope.
3. **Infinite BGM Looping & Fading:** Modulo sample indexing with smooth cosine fade-in and fade-out to prevent audio truncation or clicks.
4. **Spatial SFX Panning & Master Bus Limiter:** Constant-power stereo panning for sound effects and a master $\tanh(x)$ soft brickwall limiter to eliminate digital clipping.

## 2. Implementation Summary

### 2.1 Universal FFI Audio Decoder (`FFIAudioDecoder.ts`)
- Implemented `FFIAudioDecoder.decodeToPCM(filePath, sampleRate, channels)` in `packages/core/src/ffmpeg-ffi/FFIAudioDecoder.ts`:
  - Uses `avformat_open_input` / `avformat_find_stream_info` to inspect any container.
  - Automatically identifies audio streams, matches `avcodec_find_decoder`, and drains frames via `avcodec_send_packet` / `avcodec_receive_frame`.
  - Uses `swresample` (`swr_alloc_set_opts2`, `swr_convert`) to resample any input rate and sample format to standard **48,000 Hz, 32-bit Float Stereo (AV_SAMPLE_FMT_FLT)**.
  - Exports cleanly from `@acc/core`.

### 2.2 Dynamic Audio Ducking Engine (`DynamicAudioDuckingEngine.ts`)
- Created `packages/ir/src/renderer/audio/DynamicAudioDuckingEngine.ts`:
  - Windowed peak energy detection on the dialogue/voiceover track.
  - State machine:
    - **Attack (15ms):** Exponential ramp down to -18 dB target attenuation upon voice onset.
    - **Hold (100ms):** Retains attenuation across brief inter-word speech pauses.
    - **Release (300ms):** Smooth logarithmic recovery curve back to 0 dB unity gain when speech concludes.
  - Emits a sample-accurate `Float32Array` gain envelope curve matching timeline duration.

### 2.3 Decoupled Audio Mixer Enhancements (`DecoupledAudioMixer.ts`)
- Integrated `FFIAudioDecoder` with an in-memory `audioCache` map to eliminate repeated disk reads.
- Multi-phase mixing pipeline:
  - **Phase 1 (Dialogue):** Decodes voiceover assets into dedicated dialogue and master buffers.
  - **Phase 2 (Ducking Envelope):** Generates dynamic ducking curve from dialogue buffer if `sfx/dynamic_ducking` modifier is configured.
  - **Phase 3 (BGM):** Applies modulo sample indexing for infinite seamless looping, smooth cosine fade-in/fade-out, and multiplies by the ducking envelope.
  - **Phase 4 (SFX Panning):** Applies constant-power stereo panning ($L = \cos(\theta), R = \sin(\theta)$) to SFX clips.
  - **Phase 5 (Master Limiter):** Applies $y = \tanh(x)$ soft-clipping saturation on the final stereo bus to safeguard against digital clipping.
