# 166: Volumetric Sandwich Typography & Text Parity in Canonical IR Pipeline

## 1. Context & Motivation
During inspection of `volumetric_sandwich` rendering in `ir-render`, typography/banner text behind the subject was not appearing on screen:
1. `StoryConfigToUniversalIRAdapter` did not forward `scene.captions` into `vfx/depth3d` modifier parameters.
2. In `Depth3DPlugin`, `depthConfig` was reconstructed using an object literal that omitted `params.sandwich`, `params.parallax`, and `params.captions`.
3. `Depth3DPlugin.renderClipSpace` called `Depth3DFactory.getRenderer('volumetric_sandwich').renderFrame(...)` without passing `captions`.
4. As a result, when `bannerText` was resolved in `drawSandwichFrame`, the typography fallback to captions was empty, or custom `textColor` from `sandwich` settings was ignored in favor of a hardcoded linear gradient.

---

## 2. Technical Implementation

### A. Forwarding Captions & Sandwich Settings in `StoryConfigToUniversalIRAdapter`
- In `StoryConfigToUniversalIRAdapter.ts`, forwarded `captions: scene.captions` along with full `...scene.depth3d` properties into `videoModifiers` for `type: 'vfx/depth3d'`.

### B. Preservation of Sandwich Parameters in `Depth3DPlugin`
- Updated `Depth3DPluginParams` to explicitly include:
  - `sandwich?: any;`
  - `parallax?: any;`
  - `captions?: any[];`
  - `[key: string]: any;`
- In `Depth3DPlugin.renderClipSpace`:
  - Forwarded `sandwich: params.sandwich`, `parallax: params.parallax`, and `depthMesh: params.depthMesh` into `depthConfig`.
  - Passed `captions: params.captions` directly to `renderer.renderFrame(...)`.

### C. Typography Styling in `drawSandwichFrame`
- In `packages/effect-engines/src/depth3d/sandwich.ts`:
  - Supported custom `sandwichSettings.textColor` (e.g. `rgba(255, 230, 120, 0.95)`), falling back to high-contrast white linear gradient with drop shadow when omitted.
