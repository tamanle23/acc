## 1. Overview & Context
Prior to this task:
1. `CanvasParticleOverlayPlugin` utilized a mutable `simdEngineCache` (`SIMDParticleComputeEngine`) that stepped particles forward frame-by-frame via Euler accumulation (`pos += vel`). When scrubbing backward in the Studio NLE timeline (e.g. from frame 100 back to 45), particles maintained their advanced state, breaking deterministic rendering and causing visual drift during non-linear playback.
2. In `ShapeModifierPlugin`, shapes were positioned without an explicit center offset translation in universal `(0, 0)` top-left canvas space, leading to ambiguity upon drag-and-drop staging.

This iteration replaces Euler accumulators with a **100% stateless procedural closed-form particle field** driven by **Pts.js (`Noise`, `Pt`)** and **GSAP parametric easing (`gsap.parseEase`)**, and standardizes shape geometry origin at viewport center `(width * normX + x, height * normY + y)`.

## 2. Key Changes & Architecture

### A. Pure Closed-Form `DeterministicParticleField`
- **Location**: `packages/effect-engines/src/overlay-effects/simulators/DeterministicParticleField.ts`
- **Mathematics**:
  - Each particle index $i$ has a deterministic cycle:
    $$p_i = \text{fract}\left(\frac{\text{frame} \cdot \text{speed} + \text{offset}_i}{\text{duration}_i}\right) \in [0, 1)$$
  - Coherent spatial curl turbulence is evaluated statelessly via `pts.Noise.noise2D`:
    $$\vec{P}(i, t) = \vec{P}_{\text{base}}(i) + \vec{V}_{\text{base}}(i) \cdot p_i + \text{Noise}(\vec{P}_{\text{base}}(i) \cdot \text{freq}, t) \cdot \text{turbulence}$$
  - Opacity and scale lifecycles are shaped via GSAP parametric easing curves (`gsap.parseEase`):
    $$\alpha_i = \text{easeLife}(p_i)$$
- **Zero-Allocation**: Pre-allocated Float32 / scratch buffers and purely functional canvas drawing.
- **Presets Supported**: `snow`, `rain`, `dust`, `embers`, `fire`, `smoke`, `fog`, `bokeh`, `digital_glitch`, `vhs`, `sci_fi_hud`, and generic fallback.
- **Client & Engine Exports**: Re-exported through `@acc/effect-engines` and `@acc/effect-engines/client`.

### B. Delegation in `CanvasParticleOverlayPlugin`
- **Location**: `packages/ir/src/plugins/overlay/CanvasParticleOverlayPlugin.ts`
- Bypassed the stateful `SIMDParticleComputeEngine.update()` step.
- Delegated stream space rasterization directly to `DeterministicParticleField.render(canvasCtx, width, height, frame, params)`.
- Maintained stateless post-processing shaders (`bloom`, `anamorphicFlare`, `chromaticAberration`) applied sequentially to the rendered frame buffer.

### C. Centered Shape Placement
- **Location**: `packages/ir/src/plugins/shape/ShapeModifierPlugin.ts`
- Normalized origin translation to canvas center:
  ```typescript
  const originX = ctx.width * (params.normX ?? 0.5) + (params.x ?? 0);
  const originY = ctx.height * (params.normY ?? 0.5) + (params.y ?? 0);
  canvasCtx.translate(originX, originY);
  ```
- Geometry paths draw symmetrically centered around `(0, 0)`.
- Presets in `apps/studio/src/lib/library/stagingPresets.ts` stage shapes dead-center (`normX: 0.5, normY: 0.5, x: 0, y: 0`).
