# [102] Universal Subject-Aware Frame Breaker & Crisp Cutout

## Problem
In real-world visual storytelling projects (beyond synthetic test fixtures), subjects can appear at arbitrary scales, orientations, and vertical positions within source scenes (e.g. medium shots, seated poses, lower-third placements). The previous static framing math clamped the crop window when the subject was placed in the lower 60% of an image, causing the character's head to be placed below the top card frame border ($Y_{\text{head}} > \text{innerY}$) and resulting in zero pop-out effect. Furthermore, double smoothstep alpha blending in the segmentation post-processing softened cutout contours into hazy halos.

## Plan
1. **Universal Subject-Aware Neck Anchoring**:
   - Dynamically compute the crop origin $S_y$ directly anchored to the subject's anatomical neck line ($Y_{\text{neck}} = \text{minY} + \text{subH} \times 0.25$).
   - Dynamically calculate the crop height $S_h$ and width $S_w$ centered on the subject's horizontal centroid ($\text{subCenterX}$) while strictly preserving the source asset aspect ratio.
   - Guarantee that the upper $25\%$ of the subject extends above the card frame into the foreground 3D layer across 100% of all image compositions.
2. **Crisp Contour Alpha Thresholding**:
   - Eliminate secondary gradient degradation and clamp high-confidence foreground pixels ($\text{alpha} \ge 80$).
   - Exclude boundary edge artifacts ($x, y \ge 2$) to eliminate halo leakage.
3. **Verification**:
   - Run the 87-test regression suite `bun test autogenerated-tests`.
   - Measure actual pixel pop-out across diverse subject placements (e.g. `scene1.jpg` dinosaur $\rightarrow$ 187px popout; `scene2.jpg` lower-placed figure $\rightarrow$ 154px popout).
