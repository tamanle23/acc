## 1. Context & Motivation
Following the completion of `PipelineIRBuilder` and `DirectorPresetFactory` (Phase 3), the new auto-config system generates modern `PipelineIR` (v3.0.0) documents and `FineGrainedVideoDirectorConfig` envelopes. However, the ecosystem has extensive legacy renderers, CLI commands, and test suites expecting classic `story-config.json` (`StoryConfig`). To prevent breaking existing workflows and maintain strict zero-regression adherence, an enterprise **Adapter Pattern** was required to bi-directionally bridge between modern IR ASTs and legacy `StoryConfig` schemas.

## 2. Technical Implementation
1. **`AutoConfigLegacyBridgeAdapter` (GoF Adapter Pattern) in `packages/core/src/autoconfig/adapters/AutoConfigLegacyBridgeAdapter.ts`**:
   - `toStoryConfig(pipelineIR, directorConfig?, manifest?)`:
     - Translates `pipelineIR.metadata.title` to `storyConfig.name`.
     - Maps aspect ratio and fps into `storyConfig.settings.width`, `height`, and `platform` (`9:16` -> 1080x1920 tiktok, `16:9` -> 1920x1080 youtube, `1:1` -> 1080x1080 instagram).
     - Translates BGM track, volume, loop, and autoDucking flags.
     - Translates typography presets (`tiktok_karaoke_glow` -> `tiktok`, `3d_sandwich` -> `cinematic`, `bold_headline` -> `bold-retro`, `minimal_editorial` -> `minimal-clean`).
     - Translates 3D depth directives (`sandwich` -> `volumetric_sandwich`, `parallax_2_5d` -> `parallax_25d`, `frame_breaker` -> `frame_breaker`).
     - Maps `pipelineIR.chunks` into `storyConfig.scenes`, accurately resolving `image`, `video`, `audio`, `text`, and durations, while respecting audio-driven duration preservation.
   - `fromStoryConfig(storyConfig)`:
     - Full inverse translation reconstructing `PipelineIR` chunks and `FineGrainedVideoDirectorConfig` from any legacy `story-config.json`.
2. **Exports & Types**:
   - Re-exported `AutoConfigLegacyBridgeAdapter` via `packages/core/src/autoconfig/index.ts` and `@acc/core`.
