## 1. Context & Problem Statement
When rendering `frame_breaker`, displaying 100% of the uncropped image inside the card caused the character's head to remain completely below the top card border, preventing the 3D Pop-Out (Out of Bounds) illusion from breaking the frame. Concurrently, the user requested that the background behind the card preserve the ambient original tone rather than harsh stretching.

---

## 2. Solution & Architectural Implementation

### Character Breaker Pop-Out Engine Optimization
1. **Intelligent In-Frame Framing (`innerZoom` & `focusOffsetY`)**:
   - Card dimensions strictly match the source asset aspect ratio `imgRatio = img.width / img.height`.
   - Applied controlled inner framing (`innerZoom = 1.25` for card/comic/cinema, `1.45` for polaroid) and vertical focus anchor (`focusOffsetY = 0.16` - `0.24`).
   - `sw = img.width / innerZoom`, `sh = img.height / innerZoom`.
   - `sw / sh == innerW / innerH == imgRatio` (zero aspect distortion).
2. **Dynamic 3D Out-of-Bounds Pop-out**:
   - Affine projection scale: `scale = innerW / sw`.
   - Offset: `offsetX = innerX - sx * scale`, `offsetY = innerY - sy * scale`.
   - Because `sy > 0`, `offsetY < innerY` (shifted above the top border), projecting the character's head and upper torso **freely over the top border of the card**.
   - Body inside the card and head outside the card match 100% to the exact pixel with seamless continuity.
3. **Ambient Aspect-Preserving Backdrop**:
   - Softened background with Gaussian ambient blur and dark scrim (`rgba(10, 10, 18, 0.70)`), avoiding harsh pixel stretching while keeping the center 3D pop-out card as the sharp focal hero element.
