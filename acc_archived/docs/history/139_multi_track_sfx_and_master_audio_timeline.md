# History Log 139: Multi-Track Timeline SFX & Master Audio Track Architecture Integration

- **Task**: Implement Sound Effects (SFX) Track integration and Master Voiceover Timeline (`audioTrack`) architecture with complete 8-point sync.
- **Date**: 2026-10-05
- **Branch**: `main`

---

## 1. Context & Problem
In the Visual Storytelling Generator, timeline generation previously relied exclusively on per-scene audio slicing. This model created synchronization challenges when creators wanted:
1. A continuous, uncut master voiceover track driving visual scene pacing (similar to professional NLEs like Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve) without segmenting audio files.
2. Discrete Sound Effects (SFX) tied to individual scene cues (transitions, impacts, whooshes) or global timeline points with automatic BGM sidechain ducking.

---

1. Update `@acc/core/types` to declare `SFXClipConfig`, `AudioTrackConfig`, `SFXTrackSnapshot`, `MasterAudioTrackSnapshot`, and extend `SceneConfig`, `StorySettings`, `StoryConfig`, `ResolvedStorySettings`, and `ResolvedStoryConfig`.
2. Implement `SoundEffectsTimelineStrategy` and `MasterAudioTimingStrategy` in `@acc/core/timeline`.
3. Support explicit `scene.duration` in `getSceneDuration()` when running in master voiceover mode without scene-level audio.

5. Upgrade `VideoEditPipelineOrchestrator.ts` to remux the master voiceover track and mix multi-track SFX clips using FFmpeg adelay/amix filtergraphs.
