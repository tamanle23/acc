# History Entry 268: Studio Iteration 11 Phase 1 - LeftSidebar Staging Library & Retained Modifier Plugins

## Context & Objective
Iteration 11 aims to elevate the Studio application to an enterprise-grade UI/UX creative powerhouse. Phase 1 focuses on transforming the LeftSidebar Staging Library into a rich, categorized, and audition-capable library, strictly adhering to the architectural rule: "Everything other than assets need to be implemented with the modifiers plugin system."

## Changes Executed
1. **`packages/ir/src/plugins/shape/ShapeModifierPlugin.ts`**:
   - Implemented `ShapeModifierPlugin` implementing `ModifierPlugin<ShapeModifierParams>` for `'shape/draw'` and alias `'shape/vector'`.
   - Clip-space centered rendering supporting 10 vector geometries: `rect`, `rounded_rect`, `circle`, `pill`, `star`, `polygon`, `triangle`, `hexagon`, `cross`, and `frame`.
   - Comprehensive styling: linear & radial gradients, drop shadows (`shadowColor`, `shadowBlur`), border stroke dashes, and corner radiuses.
   - Guaranteed full compatibility with canvas transform handles and affine matrix stacking.

2. **`packages/ir/src/plugins/sfx/ProceduralSFXModifierPlugin.ts`**:
   - Implemented `ProceduralSFXModifierPlugin` implementing `ModifierPlugin<ProceduralSFXModifierParams>` for `'sfx/procedural'`.
   - Pure Float32 DSP synthesis primitives (`synthClick`, `synthPop`, `synthBeep`, `synthSubDrop`) utilizing deterministic noise math (`DETERMINISTIC-HANDSHAKE-BACKPRESSURE`) without non-deterministic `Math.random()`.
   - In-place timeline audio mixdown hook `processAudio` and standalone PCM buffer generator `generateAudioBuffer`.

3. **`packages/ir/src/plugins/index.ts`, `packages/ir/src/client.ts`, `packages/ir/src/plugins/clientRegistry.ts`**:
   - Registered `ShapeModifierPlugin` under `'shape/draw'` and `'shape/vector'`.
   - Registered `ProceduralSFXModifierPlugin` under `'sfx/procedural'`.
   - Maintained 100% browser-safe isomorphic export definitions.

4. **`apps/studio/src/lib/library/sfxBank.ts`**:
   - Curated 104 distinct sound effect definitions across 6 banks:
     - Whooshes & Swishes (20 items)
     - Impacts & Booms (20 items)
     - UI Clicks & Pops (20 items)
     - Risers & Tension (15 items)
     - Cyber & Glitch (15 items)
     - Ambient & Drones (14 items)
   - Provided `convertSFXToPreset` helper for drag-and-drop timeline placement.

5. **`apps/studio/src/lib/library/stagingPresets.ts` & `apps/studio/src/components/LeftSidebar.tsx`**:
   - Extended categories with `'video'` and `'audio'` footages.
   - Enhanced `DraggablePresetCard` with poster thumbnails, duration badges, and audio BPM/score tags.
   - Integrated inline Web Audio audition button for instant playback of SFX and Audio Footages.

6. **`autogenerated-tests/studio_phase1_leftsidebar_retained_plugins.test.ts`**:
   - 11 unit/integration tests verifying geometry rendering, gradient styling, Float32 buffer synthesis, bank sizes, preset conversion, and catalog integrity.
