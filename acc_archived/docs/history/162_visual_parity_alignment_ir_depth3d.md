# [162] Visual Parity Alignment: True Subject Cutout & Backdrop Plate for IR Depth3D

- **Status:** `[x] Completed`
- **Date:** 2026-10-10
- **Scope:** `packages/effect-engines/src/depth3d/`, `packages/ir/src/adapters/`, `packages/ir/src/plugins/vfx/`, `packages/ir/src/renderer/`

---

## 1. Context & Problem Statement
Even though the 3D depth renderer was invoked in `ir-render`, the visual output differed noticeably from `renderStoryVideo`. Specifically:
1. `renderStoryVideo` (via `renderDepth3DScene`) loaded cached subject cutouts (`scene1_subject.png` etc.) and companion bounding boxes from `autogenerated/depth-3d`, enabling the character to pop out of the frame. In contrast, `Depth3DPlugin` was passing `subjectImg: null`, forcing the generic ellipse fallback.
2. `renderStoryVideo` synthesized a dedicated darkened background plate (`bgCanvas`) preserving original 100% aspect ratio letterbox/pillarbox behind the floating card. `Depth3DPlugin` was missing this background plate.
3. Modifier pre-flight (`execute`) was not being triggered on sequences in `IRMasterRenderer`, preventing pre-loading of AI segmentations or cutouts.

---

## 2. Changes Made
1. **`packages/effect-engines/src/depth3d/index.ts`**:
   - Implemented and exported `resolveSubjectCutout(imagePath, depthConfig)` to resolve cached subject cutouts (`*_subject.png`, checksum matches, companion `.json` bounding boxes) and auto-segmentations.
   - Exported `checkExists` and `getStoryRootAndCacheDir`.
2. **`packages/ir/src/adapters/StoryConfigToUniversalIRAdapter.ts`**:
   - Forwarded complete `scene.depth3d` configuration and `visualSrc` image path to `vfx/depth3d` modifier parameters.
3. **`packages/ir/src/plugins/vfx/Depth3DPlugin.ts`**:
   - Pre-resolves and caches subject cutouts via `resolveSubjectCutout` during `execute()`.
   - Synthesizes and caches the dimmed cinematic backdrop plate (`bgCanvas`) preserving exact image aspect ratios.
   - Passes `subjectImg`, `subjectBBox`, and `bgCanvas` directly into `Depth3DFactory.getRenderer(mode).renderFrame()`.
4. **`packages/ir/src/renderer/IRMasterRenderer.ts`**:
   - Added pre-flight execution loop calling `plugin.execute()` on all track and sequence modifiers before frame stepping begins.
