# Studio Implementation Plan - Iteration 71: Unified Audio Mixer & Hardware Sample-Rate Auto-Negotiation

## Problem & Design Alignment
In studio preview, audio playback through `WebAudioEngine` suffered from:
1. **Pitch Shift & Clock Drift**: `WebAudioEngine` initialized `AudioContext` at 44.1 kHz, while `IRPreviewEngine`'s mixer operated at 48.0 kHz. Slices were copied directly into mismatching audio buffers, resulting in an 8.8% pitch-down and ~5.3s/min lag behind the video canvas playhead.
2. **Ducking Flutter at Slice Boundaries**: `IRPreviewEngine.renderAudioSlice()` evaluated sidechain ducking in isolated 5-second windows without prior envelope state, causing audible volume fluttering at chunk boundaries.
3. **Decoder Inconsistency**: `AudioAssetCache` used DOM `AudioContext.decodeAudioData`, diverging from master export PCM decoders.

Following Iteration 86 (Option 2), this plan implements the studio client changes:
- Auto-negotiate hardware sample rates directly with `AudioContext.sampleRate`.
- Synchronize `IRPreviewEngine` and `UniversalAudioMixer` sample rates to match the browser's audio hardware clock.
- Leverage the pre-computed timeline ducking envelope from `@acc/ir` for smooth, flutter-free preview playback across chunk boundaries.

## Implementation Steps
1. **`apps/studio/src/lib/audio/WebAudioEngine.ts`**:
   - In `initContext()`, query native `this.ctx.sampleRate` and synchronize `this.mixer.setSampleRate(this.ctx.sampleRate)`.
   - In `attachPreviewEngine(engine)`: synchronize `engine.setAudioSampleRate(this.ctx.sampleRate)`.
   - In `startPlayback()`: assert matching sample rates between `this.mixer` and `this.ctx` before allocating `createBuffer()`.
2. **`apps/studio/src/renderer/IRPreviewEngine.ts`**:
   - Expose `public setAudioSampleRate(sampleRate: number): void` which forwards to `this.audioMixer.setSampleRate()`.
3. **Verification**:
   - Author formal tests in `autogenerated-tests/unified_audio_muxing_option2.test.ts`.
   - Verify zero pitch drift and continuous ducking across slice boundaries.
   - Run `pnpm --filter @acc/studio build`.
