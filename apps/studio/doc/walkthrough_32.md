## 1. Architectural Changes Overview

In this iteration, we resolved the boundary coupling between `@acc/ir` and `@acc/studio`:
1. **Mathematical Motion Evaluators in `@acc/ir`**:
   - `BezierMath.ts`, `TemporalKeyframeEvaluator.ts`, and `DeterministicMotionEvaluator.ts` relocated from `packages/ir/src/renderer/preview/` to `packages/ir/src/motion/`.
   - Exported directly from `@acc/ir` for zero-alloc deterministic keyframe and transform evaluation.
2. **Preview Engine Relocated to `@acc/studio`**:
   - `IRPreviewEngine` relocated to `apps/studio/src/renderer/preview/IRPreviewEngine.ts`.
   - Exported via `@acc/studio` and `apps/studio/src/renderer/preview/index.ts`.
   - Interfaces directly with Studio's `AssetTextureCache`, `IRCanvasCompositor`, and `UniversalAudioMixer`.
3. **Universal Background Sidecar Architecture**:
   - Established `StudioSidecarWorker` (`apps/studio/src/workers/preview-sidecar.worker.ts`) and `StudioSidecarCoordinator` (`apps/studio/src/renderer/preview/sidecar/StudioSidecarCoordinator.ts`).
   - Supports background off-thread preflight (`plugin.execute()`) for all modifier types.
   - Includes proximity-based priority queuing ($\pm 3$s scrubbing window), non-blocking progressive rendering, task aborts on sequence deletion, and zero-stub in-process fallback in Node/Bun test environments.
   - Deprecated and removed bespoke `apps/studio/src/workers/visionWorker.ts` and `apps/studio/src/lib/vision/StudioVisionPipelineManager.ts`.
