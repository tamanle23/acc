# Walkthrough 33 - Depth3DPlugin Sequence Asset Resolution and Inference Fix

## Summary of Changes
- **Isomorphic Depth3D Plugin** (`packages/ir/src/plugins/vfx/Depth3DPlugin.ts`):
  - In `execute()`: resolves media path from `params.imagePath`, sequence `assetId`, `context.assets`, and `sequence.src`.
  - Broadened `needsDepth` condition to include all 17+ 3D depth modes (`frame_breaker`, `parallax_25d`, `parallax_dolly`, `volumetric_sandwich`, `sandwich`, `depth_map_3d`, `mesh_displacement`, `depth_heatmap`, etc.).
  - Caches resolved neural subject cutout and depth maps under candidate keys `[effectiveImagePath, params.imagePath, assetId, seq?.id, seq?.src]`.
  - In `renderClipSpace()`: queries caches against all candidate keys, guaranteeing instant hits during frame playback even when `params.imagePath` is omitted.
- **Effect Engines** (`packages/effect-engines/src/depth3d/index.ts`):
  - Updated `resolveDepthMapData` to export `srcCanvas: dCanvas` along with `depthCanvas` and `depthData`.
- **Sidecar Coordinator & Worker** (`apps/studio/src/renderer/`):
  - `IRPreviewEngine.worker.ts`: forwards `payload.assets` to `context.assets`.
  - `StudioSidecarCoordinator.ts`: injects `assets` from timeline into `executeModifier` calls during `preflightTimeline()`, binds `imagePath: mod.params?.imagePath || effectiveImagePath`, and guards `new Worker` in non-browser Node/Bun test environments.
- **Verification Tests**:
  - Added `autogenerated-tests/depth3d_inference_resolution.test.ts`.
  - Verified `autogenerated-tests/studio_sidecar_coordinator.test.ts`, `autogenerated-tests/depth_diagnostic_preview.test.ts`, and `autogenerated-tests/ir_renderer_web_server_parity.test.ts`.
