## 2. Key Architecture Components

  7. `NetworkPlexusPlugin` (`time_space/network_plexus`): Proximity-connected particle plexus.
  8. `AudioWaveformPlugin` (`time_space/audio_waveform`): Radial FFT waveform ring with harmonic ripples.
  9. `AudioSpectrumPlugin` (`time_space/audio_spectrum`): Dynamic equalizer bars with gravitational peak-hold.
  10. `SuperformulaPlugin` (`time_space/superformula`): Johan Gielis parametric superformula geometry.
  11. `EnergyShieldPlugin` (`time_space/energy_shield`): Hexagonal energy barrier lattice.
