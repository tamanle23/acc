# Walkthrough - Iteration 37 / Milestone 31: VideoIR Sequence & Track ID Governance, Hierarchical Authoring, and Referential Integrity

## Overview
Successfully implemented and verified the identity, naming, and referential integrity governance model across VideoIR and the Studio commands pipeline:
- **Explicit Identity vs. Display Separation**: Formalized `id` (mandatory, globally unique system key) and `name` (optional, human-readable display label) on tracks and sequences.
- **Universal Authoring Engine (`VideoIRAuthoringEngine`)**: Centralized in `@acc/ir` (`packages/ir/src/authoring/VideoIRAuthoringEngine.ts`), producing deterministic, track-move immune IDs (`seq_<modality>_<slug>_<globalIndex>`).
- **Global Monotonic High-Water Mark**: Maintained in `doc.meta.nextSequenceIndex`, ensuring IDs always increment forward across clip additions, splits, and deletions.
- **Strict Referential Integrity**: Two-pass validation in `VideoIRSchemaValidator.validate()` rejecting duplicate sequence IDs globally, dangling relational targets (`after`, `syncWith`, `matchLengthOf`, `linkedSequenceId`, `overlapTarget`, `outgoingSeqId`), and self-referencing links.
- **Studio Command Integration**: Wired `SplitSequenceCommand`, `DuplicateSequenceCommand`, `AddSequenceCommand`, and `AddLinkedAVSequenceCommand` to the authoring engine.
