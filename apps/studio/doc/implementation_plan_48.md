## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Formulation

Expanding on the discrete `time_space/*` architecture established in Iteration 53, this iteration implements **6 advanced generative spatio-temporal effect families** and expands the Studio showcase environment into multi-domain scientific and artistic workflows.

### Objectives:
1. Implement mathematical state-machine generators in `TimeSpaceRegistry.ts`:
   - `cymatic_chladni`: Acoustic nodal standing wave patterns.
   - `strange_attractor`: Clifford & De Jong continuous chaos bifurcations.
   - `relativistic_blackhole`: Gravitational lensing, Einstein rings, and Keplerian Doppler beaming.
   - `topographic_contour`: Multi-octave curl turbulence isoline terrain contours.
   - `hyperbolic_poincare`: Non-Euclidean Poincaré disk conformal Möbius tessellations.
   - `kinetic_harmonograph`: Multi-pendulum damped harmonic epicycles.
2. Implement 6 corresponding discrete plugins under `packages/ir/src/plugins/time-space/` inheriting from `BaseTimeSpacePlugin<TParams>`.
3. Synchronize `VideoIR` types and runtime schema validation.
4. Expose all 6 plugins in Studio Inspector (`modifierCatalog.ts`).
5. Introduce 2 rich multi-track production demo projects in Studio:
   - `quantumPhysicsDemoIR.ts` (Quantum Physics & Cymatics Laboratory)
   - `astroTelemetryDemoIR.ts` (Astroscientific Deep Space & Black Hole Singularity)
6. Expand Studio Titlebar demo switcher and `IRStateManager` to support all 6 demo projects (`default`, `scifi`, `broadcast`, `visualizer`, `quantum`, `astro`).
7. Formal verification via automated unit tests in `autogenerated-tests/`.
