## 2. Key Architecture Components

### 2.1 Six Mathematical Effect Families (`@acc/core-isomorphic`)
1. **Quantum Cymatics (`cymatic_chladni`)**:
   - High-order Chladni standing acoustic resonance wave equation:
     $$w(x, y) = a \cos(n \pi x) \cos(m \pi y) + b \cos(m \pi x) \cos(n \pi y)$$
   - Deterministic nodal particle congregation and energy-field isoline rendering.
2. **Chaotic Strange Attractors (`strange_attractor`)**:
   - Clifford and De Jong non-linear iterative maps with Lyapunov chaotic bifurcations:
     $$x_{k+1} = \sin(a y_k) + c \cos(a x_k), \quad y_{k+1} = \sin(b x_k) + d \cos(b y_k)$$
   - Burn-in pre-iteration cycle and multi-point phase-space projection.
3. **Relativistic Singularity & Einstein Rings (`relativistic_blackhole`)**:
   - Kerr metric black hole accretion simulation featuring gravitational photon sphere deflection, Einstein ring lamination, and relativistic Keplerian Doppler beaming ($I_{\text{obs}} = I_0 \delta^{3+\alpha}$).
4. **Topographic Isolines & Turbulence Contours (`topographic_contour`)**:
   - Multi-octave curl turbulence noise terrain with deterministic marching contour isolines and index contour elevation styling.
5. **Hyperbolic Poincaré Conformal Disk (`hyperbolic_poincare`)**:
   - Conformal non-Euclidean geometry with Poincaré disk boundary containment ($r < 1.0$), Möbius transform rotations, and orthogonal circle arcs.
6. **Kinetic Harmonograph Epicyclic Pendulum (`kinetic_harmonograph`)**:
   - Quad-pendulum damped harmonic oscillator dynamics tracing complex Lissajous epicyclic paths:
     $$x(t) = A_1 \sin(f_1 t + p_1) e^{-d_1 t} + A_2 \sin(f_2 t + p_2) e^{-d_2 t}$$
